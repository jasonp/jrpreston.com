Placeholder for the "confusing complexity for difficulty" essay, also living in `_reference/drafts/` and slated for M5.

The shape: complexity is when there are many moving parts. Difficulty is when the decision costs you something. A complicated decision can feel hard because of all the analysis, but what makes it actually hard is the cost. Most of the things we treat as hard problems are merely complicated, and most of the things we treat as merely complicated turn out to be hard once you actually have to choose.
