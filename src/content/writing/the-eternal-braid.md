This is a placeholder for the eternal braid essay, which exists in draft form in the `_reference/drafts/` folder and will be revived as part of the M5 archive migration.

The shape of the essay is about how the act of writing about thinking changes the thinking, and how that change loops back into the writing. It's an old idea — Hofstadter, Borges, the strange loop — but I want to write about it from the perspective of someone who has kept a journal continuously since 2001 and watched themselves change in real time across the archive.
