If I remember correctly, it cost be approximately $300 to buy the full-on monkeys-and-organ grinders AppleCare protection plan for my MacBook Pro. I dislike spending money, but I was well aware that if my laptop *didn't* break at some point in the next three years, I should probably start playing golf in thunderstorms.

Unlike my friend Ethan's [recent experience](http://rabidspacedog.com/2007/09/17/the-weebles-and-wobbles-of-rules-brought-to-you-by-the-apple-ipod/) with Apple customer service, my call today went smoothly enough. I followed [his advice](http://rabidspacedog.com/2007/09/17/how-to-get-good-technical-support/) on getting good tech support (we talked about lunch breaks), and I don't know if he did anything particular for me, but it ended up being a no-hassle call.

For the past week or so my laptop has been randomly shutting down at 40%-20% battery levels. Not a "need power" warning--just shutting down. He had be bring up some panel of information and apparently some of the numbers for my battery are...incorrect.

So they're shipping me a new, $120 battery for free. I drop the old one in the box and send it back.

$180 to go...
