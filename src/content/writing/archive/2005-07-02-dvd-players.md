My friends Jeremy, Jacob and Adam have been watching the Sopranos in my basement with me this evening, but we've been playing it with the PS2. And every other scene there's this weird hum from the audio...why? Because the connectors from the PS2 to the TV are messed up.

Now, I would normally use the Xbox to play a DVD, because it's connected via HD cable anyway, but the problem is that I can only find half of the DVD kit at one time. It's a brilliant ploy from Microsoft--don't include DVD support, and then sell a kit that you can't keep plugged in if you're playing a four player game. If anyone has lost their controller, and doesn't have any use for their little reciever, feel free to send it my way.
