I'm at the SDMA Market to the Max conference at Bell Harbor today, and as Steve Broback [already noted on Twitter](http://twitter.com/sbroback/status/1311298060), there are hardly any laptops in the room.

The unfortunate corollary to that: no plugs, either. Last time I was here (Gnomedex 8.0) you couldn't go five feet without finding a power strip, which is awesome. As it is, I'll have to make do with the awkwardly place one on the end of the row.
