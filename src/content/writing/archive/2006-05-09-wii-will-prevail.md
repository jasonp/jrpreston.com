I am rooting for Nintendo so hard. Amidst the ridiculous "console wars" between Microsoft and Sony, it's so wonderful to see Nintendo putter along misnaming its consoles and introducing by far the coolest shit.

They [just announced](http://www.engadget.com/2006/05/09/nintendo-shows-off-wii-remote/) at E3 that the new-style motion sensor controller has a speaker and mic built in for ambience and voice-activated gameplay. They also announced a classic version of the controller for the less adventurous gamer.

So right now, here's my list of why I'm buying a Wii:  

  
2.  It's gonna be waaaay cheaper than the other consoles
  
4.  It will sport, in my opinion, the best games catalogue: every nintento title ever
  
6.  It has a sweet new motion-sensitive, voice-activating, ambient-sound-producing controller
  
8.  It's comparatively tiny, and it doesn't try to be a computer
