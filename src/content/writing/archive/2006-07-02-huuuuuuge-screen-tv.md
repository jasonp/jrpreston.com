Last night, just for fun, my friends and I watched Batman Begins on a screen the size of a Queensize bedsheet.

Basically, we tacked the sheet to the wall and set up my projector just to see how it would look ridiculously large. It looks *nice*.

I really couldn't tell you there were any problems. It looked great, even on a wrinkled sheet. When I get this thing set up and roof-mounted with a proper screen, it's going to look gorgeous.
