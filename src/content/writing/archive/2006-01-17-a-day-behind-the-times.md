I always wondered about how Sussex, which in every other way had [terrible internet access and networking setup](http://www.jason-preston.com/?p=188), never seemed to run out of bandwidth. I wondered how a school that coulnd't always afford to replace broken shower heads and light bulbs found money to pipe in enough bandwidth for 500 skypers to simultaneously leave me room to download WoW videos at over 100 k/second.
