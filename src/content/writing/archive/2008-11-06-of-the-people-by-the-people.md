[Image removed — original hosted on WordPress]I have to say that this little widget is one of the coolest things I've found on [change.gov](http://www.change.gov/). When Obama says that he wants the people to participate in their government, he apparently means it.

People have always been free to submit their own ideas to the government in power, of course, but I don't think I've ever seen it so freely *welcomed* and made so easy to do.

This is going to be cool.
