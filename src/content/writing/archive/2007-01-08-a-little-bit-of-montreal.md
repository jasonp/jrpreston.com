Ever since Fred Wilson [posted a song](http://avc.blogs.com/a_vc/2006/12/of_montreal.html) from the upcoming album [Hissing Fauna, Are You The Destroyer?](http://www.amazon.com/Hissing-Fauna-Are-You-Destroyer/dp/B000KWZ94U/sr=11-1/qid=1168303441/ref=sr_11_1/103-8689663-6967055) by Of Montreal, I have been growing addicted to this band.

I've already put in my pre-order for this album, because it is an amazing piece of work.

Since I've been away from my apartment for the past month, just about, I have been relying on my iPod and whatever new music I've bought for my listening. The iPod is great, but I really prefer to listen to my music on speakers instead of headphones.

As a result, I don't have a very large selection of music on my new laptop. I bought the new Weird Al CD, and I got some Rolling Stones music for Christmas, so there's those, and the Of Montreal piece. It should say something that I'm not bored of the selection yet.

I highly recommend the album. If you're curious, go play the song on Fred's blog to see what they sound like.
