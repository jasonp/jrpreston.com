Matt Blumberg coins the term in [his post](http://onlyonce.blogs.com/onlyonce/2006/01/new_media_deal_.html) over at the OnlyOnce blog.

He is also describing the way the web has changed, which is what I was trying to put my finger on in my post on [how to use the new web](http://www.jason-preston.com/?p=361).

The difference is that he is describing how the web is changing and I am describing how to use the internet in its new form. He sums up the changes well in this quote:  

> All these new web services of late have transformed the mass Internet from a read platform to a read/write platform, so now everyone can have a say in things.

  
It's a good post, so you should read it if you're interested in this sort of thing.
