I'm very happy to say that [my post](http://eatsleeppublish.com/why-the-future-of-news-brands-hinges-on-net-neutrality/) about net neutrality and the future of newsbrands [made it to the Voices section](http://voices.allthingsd.com/20090127/why-the-future-of-news-brands-hinges-on-net-neutrality/) in AllThingsD, the incredibly forward-thinking and WordPress-based digital arm of the Wall Street Journal.

As a result, Eat Sleep Publish is currently being crushed under the weight of zillions of eyeballs. At least, I assume that's what's going on, because Google analytics doesn't give me live traffic stats, and I can't think of any other reason why the site would load like a snail and the back-end won't even let me approve comments, let alone make a new post today.

Oh, if only I were *really* that popular...
