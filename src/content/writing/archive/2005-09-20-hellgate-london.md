[Hellgate: London](http://www.hellgatelondon.com/) caught my eye several months ago when[PC Gamer](http://www.pcgamer.com/) ran a feature article on it. Basically, the team that created Diablo essentially left Blizzard en masse to form a new group intent on re-defining gaming again.

I was upset at the time, because the article overlooked the fact that Hellgate: London was, in design, essentially the same as [Deus Ex](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/redirect?link_code=ur2&camp=1789&tag=flickergaming-20&creative=9325&path=tg/detail/-/B00003OPES/qid=1127240208/sr=8-2/ref=pd_bbs_unbuck_2?v=glance%26s=videogames%26n=229534)[Image removed], which is probably one of my favorite games of all time. But if I can look past the slight insult to a game that really deserves some credit, Hellgate: London keeps looking better and better.

For those of you unfamiliar with Deus Ex, HG London is somewhat like a first person Diablo--you wander the levels and collect items, killing your enemies along the way. The innovations they claim are that the first-person genre is introduced to an inventory system (in Deus Ex) and that the levels might be randomly constructed (*that* would be damn cool).

In any case, the official site just [released some new screenshots](http://www.hellgatelondon.com/downloads.html#screenshots). They look cool. And I'm excited.
