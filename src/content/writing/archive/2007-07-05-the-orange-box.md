I know it's a little bit early to get all excited about Half-Life 2 Episode 2, but I'm excited anyway. I've been a Valve fanboy for years, ever since my friend tricked me into playing one of those games with "graphics that look like triangles," which turned out to be Half-Life.

Since then I've bought and played through the sequels, the expansions, and I still play Counter-Strike. I met Gabe Newell, who has a charming habit of needing to prop his glasses back up on his nose. And now I want The Orange Box (for PC of course):
