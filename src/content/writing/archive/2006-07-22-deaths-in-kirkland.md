In case you didn't hear about the [story in Kirkland](http://seattletimes.nwsource.com/html/localnews/2003144181_kirkfire22e.html), it turns out the massive house fire was arson, the fire set by a neighbor who claims he woke up in pools of blood next to the deceased.

Apparently, he showered himself off then torched the house hoping to hide the evidence.

What's really interesting is that the Judge wouldn't let the Seattle Times photograph the suspect's face, so the Times grabbed his headshot from MySpace.
