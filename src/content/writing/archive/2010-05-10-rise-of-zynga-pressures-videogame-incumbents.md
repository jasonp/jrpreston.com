[Rise of Zynga Pressures Videogame Incumbents](http://online.wsj.com/article/SB10001424052748704173804575233390919534652.html?mod=WSJ_Tech_LEADTop)

> Faced with shrinking sales in console and computer games, big game publishers are now starting to wade into social games themselves. EA last November acquired Zynga rival Playfish Ltd. in a deal valued at as much as $400 million. THQ Inc., best known for wrestling and fighting games, has created a development team focused on social games and plans to release three games this year that can be played through Facebook.

We're also looking at the rise of ad-powered video games.
