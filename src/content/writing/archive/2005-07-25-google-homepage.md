Remember when I posted about how [customized Google was better than My Yahoo!](http://www.jason-preston.com/?p=35)? I had one real complaint about how the Google homepage was set up, and my prayers have been answered.

[Customized Google](http://www.google.com/ig) earlier today rolled out an entirely new set of functionality, now allowing you to enter any custom RSS feed for a selection of headlines. They changed the setup menu so that you select various items from a sidebar, and then move them around the page -- as far as I can tell the only things they removed was the "driving directions" tab, and the extra spacing at the top (which I liked, unfortuantely).

In any case, go nuts. [Add my feed](http://feeds.feedburner.com/JasonPreston) ;)
