I suppose it's odd that I almost never blog about my work, since my work essentially revolves around the blogosphere. In fact, it's probably *because* of that that I don't usually have much to say about work - I tend to just blab about RSS this or podcasting that and simply leave it there.

But, regardless, I'm going to be working the Blog Business Summit the next few days with my wonderful co-workers ([here's one](http://teresacentric.typepad.com/)) up in beautiful Seattle, and I'm really excited about it.

There are going to be zillions of [really cool people](http://www.blogbusinesssummit.com/attendees/) haning about talking about some [really interesting stuff](http://www.blogbusinesssummit.com/sessions/). If you're into the blogosphere and you aren't excited about it, you damn well should be.
