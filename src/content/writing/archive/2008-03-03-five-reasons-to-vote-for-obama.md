Tomorrow brings another primary. I have not participated overly much in the "well oiled machinery" of the Obama campaign, but I know that [Teresa](http://teresacentric.com) and [Baratunde](http://baratunde.com/blog/) are both being active and awesome proponents of his campaign.

My contribution will be a blog post (oooh, big spender, me!).

Here are five reasons to vote for Obama, in no particular order or measure of importance:

-   He understands the internet. Read his stances on [technology issues](http://www.barackobama.com/issues/technology/).
  
-   Such a huge portion of his campaign money has come from **normal, individual donors**, which means that as a politician he is [beholden primarily to the public](http://baratunde.com/blog/archives/2008/02/how_obama_is_threatening_peoples_money_and_why_its_important_for_democracy.html).
  
-   Despite what you've heard, Obama is an [experienced politician and an experienced leader](http://www.dailykos.com/storyonly/2008/1/6/12495/62063). And he has served as an elected official for **longer than Hillary Clinton**. In other words, his job has always been on the line.
  
-   By all accounts, Obama is a [really intelligent, curious man](http://blog.pmarca.com/2008/03/an-hour-and-a-h.html). It is far more important to have someone who can evaluate and react with innovation than someone who just knows the playbook.
  
-   He is authentic. I have not yet heard him give the answer he thinks someone wants to hear instead of the answer he thinks is correct.

And seriously, I know it's been said, but if you're worried about his ability to run something big, take a look at his campaign.
