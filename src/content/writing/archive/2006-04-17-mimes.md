Today is the last day I leave my camera in my room instead of taking it with me. I had to go back up to get it just because this was such an odd thing to find in the quad.

Apparently, there was a troupe of mimes performing for several hours in a re-enactment of the Hurrican Katrina disaster.

Watching a mass miming can be a pretty odd experience. It's simultaneously fascinating and disturbing.
