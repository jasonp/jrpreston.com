I think I'm still within my 2-week free trial period for Netflix, which I just signed up for when I got back to campus.

So far, it's phenominal. I just got my second round of DVDs in the mail today, *Sky Captain and the World of Tomorrow* and *The Fisher King*, two movies that I've been meaning to see for a while, but haven't had the time or the inclination to really pursue renting them (Blockbuster doesn't carry *The Fisher King* in stock).

But when they're mailed to me for free, almost instantaneously...how can I resist?
