[Image removed]I have always been a gadget man. For some reason, things like palm pilots, mp3 players, and laser pens have made me drool the way chocolate just cannot.

What do I get to drool about today? The fact that the [Samsung t809](http://www.t-mobile.com/products/overview.asp?phoneid=611994&class=phone) just became available for T-mobile in the US.

This is part of the first wave of US phones that has what they're touting as "removable memory." What that means is that you can stick an SD card in your phone (the kind of thing that goes in most digital cameras) and get anywhere bewteen 32 megs and 2 gigabytes of data storage on your phone. And play mp3s. So much for mp3 players, yeah?

Oh, and this baby's also a voice recorder, a 1.3 megapixel camera, and a "web broswer." QUIERO!
