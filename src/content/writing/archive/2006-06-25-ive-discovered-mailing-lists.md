It seems odd that it tooks me so long to figure out what mailing lists are, and how potentially cool they can be.

But just recently I joined a mailing list called [css-discuss](http://www.css-discuss.org/) because it's a great resource for learning how little I know about css and getting berated for poor coding on my sites.

Fundamentally, though, the idea is pretty sweet. It's like a forum that comes to your inbox.
