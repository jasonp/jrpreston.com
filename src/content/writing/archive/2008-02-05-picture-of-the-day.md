The name is a bit of a misnomer since I plan to post pictures here only on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays.

The pictures will all be pictures that I have taken and find interesting. I'll try to include the metadata for all of the photos so you can see what kind of equipment and settings I was using.

All of the photos you see here will be available to buy either in print for or as a hi-res JPEG file that you can print or use yourself in any way you want.
