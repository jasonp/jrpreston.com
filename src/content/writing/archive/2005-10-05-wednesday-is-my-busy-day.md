At home (well, at Oxy) I'm used to classes being either two or three days a week for a total of three hours. Usually, that means I have a Mon-Wed-Fri schedule and a separate Tue-Thur schedule. In fact, the past three semesters I've had Tuesday and Thursday completely off.

Over here, the classes are generally divided into two parts: one lecture, one seminar. As a result, I have one weird, asymetrical schedule, and there's no way I'm going to keep track of it myself. The most interesting thing is that since I'm an international student, I think the administration here decided I was the unlucky sucker who got stuck in the unpopular seminar times. Probably because I basically have nobody to complain to.

In any case, every Wednesday I have a seminar at 9am, a lecture at 11, a film screening from 2-5 and a separate film screening from 4-7 (yeah, I know. So does the professor...it's OK).

But I got up so early this morning that after my first class I had time to talk to Ben before he went to bed the night before...

As a result, I have basically no energy (or will to write), so I'm going to postpone most of the entries I had lined up in my head until tomorrow.
