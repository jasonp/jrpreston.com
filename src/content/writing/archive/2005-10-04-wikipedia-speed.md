Back when [Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/redirect?link_code=ur2&camp=1789&tag=flickergaming-20&creative=9325&path=tg/detail/-/0439784549/qid=1128446767/sr=2-1/ref=pd_bbs_b_2_1?v=glance%26s=books)[Image removed] was released, I wanted to spend some time checking Wikipedia to see how quickly new Harry Potter terms appeared with definitions. Unfortunately, I ended up spending too much time reading the book, so when I went to check, all of the definitions were already up.

But, still fascinated with Wikipedia in a quiet sort of way, I'm sorely tempted to go around incorrecting various definitions (changing phrases like "as the probability approaches 1" to "as the probability approaches 100") and checking back to see how long it takes them to get fixed.

I'm resisting the urge.
