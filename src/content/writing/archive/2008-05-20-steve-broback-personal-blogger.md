It's always been a mystery to me why one of the first people to really get blogging took so long to set up a personal blog. I've watched my boss, [Steve Broback](http://www.stevebroback.com), try to fit all kinds of insane (but interesting) topics under the umbrella of business blogging or social media just so he can get away with posting it.

Now he doesn't need to go searching for excuses anymore.

For your regular dose of Broback, go ahead and [add his feed](http://feeds.feedburner.com/BrobacksBlog) to your reader.
