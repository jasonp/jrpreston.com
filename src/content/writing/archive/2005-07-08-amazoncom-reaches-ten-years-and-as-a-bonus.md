When I went to visit Amazon.com today they had a 10year old birthday notice on their page. The most interesting part was this:

"As a special thanks for supporting their work, over 20 actors, musicians, athletes and authors will each personally make a deliver directly to a customer's doorstep between July 7 and July 16. Someone will open his or her door to find Harrison Ford with *Raiders of the Lost Ark*. Moby will deliver his recently released CD *Hotel*. Anna Kournikova will deliver something from Adidas, and Jason Alexander will show up with a *Seinfeld* DVD."
