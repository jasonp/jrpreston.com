Occasionally I lose track of little things on my site like blogrolls, which, according to a [recent poll](http://www.jason-preston.com/index.php/2007/07/31/who-uses-a-blogroll-anymore/), I should actually pay attention to because people use it.

I've been reading Buttonmashing.com now for over a year, and even though Tony has diluted himself with two other excellent contributors, I'm still compelled to read it daily. It's a sin that I haven't had him in my blogroll.

Now he is. Go [read his blog](http://www.buttonmashing.com).
