-   At the beach yesterday I was quietly minding my own business when the right side of my face started to sweel up for no particular reason. It wasn't a mass swelling, but like might happen if I'd been stung by a bee, and I was allergic to them. I wasn't, and I'm not allergic to bees - and we couldn't find any sort of bite behind it at all. I wouldn't mention it except that it's swelling back up today. What does that?
