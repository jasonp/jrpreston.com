I've been enjoying a lot of stat padding recently because I am the [number 1 search result](http://www.google.com/search?hl=en&q=tv+links+replacement&btnG=Google+Search) for tv links replacement on Google. This is because mere hours after finding out about TV links' demise, I posted a short list of sites people could use now that the old classic was offline, AND nobody more popular than me posted about it, either.

I know that this is a temporary blessing. That sooner or later people will stop searching for replacement sites since they'll know what they are already.

I can only hope that a few of the people cruising through will add [my RSS feed](http://feeds.feedburner.com/jasonpreston) to their reader or decide that my blog is cool enough to bookmark and check back later.
