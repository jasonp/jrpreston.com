I'd like to underline a point (of disagreement) I made a week ago after reading [Seth Godin's e-book on blogging](http://sethgodin.typepad.com/seths_blog/2005/09/whos_there_the_.html), in which he states that if you write something interesting enough, often enough, people will start to read it.

In a stunning counter-example, I have been writing reasonable interesting things for three months, and my readership is, as best as I can tell, static. ;)
