I know I'm a geek because I get Laptop Envy pretty easily. My boss has a brand new MacBook Pro on the way, and my roommate Jacob has a new ultraportable (or at least he will if he can manage to be home for the scheduled delivery time).

Couple this with the fact that I just got my laptop signed by Levar Burton...it makes me really want to stow this thing in a display case and use it as an excuse to blow WAY too much money on a new laptop.

OK, now that I've said it, I think I can go back to ignoring my impulses. Thanks for indulging.
