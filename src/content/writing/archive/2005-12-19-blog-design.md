How do I know that my blog design is good? Because it makes me want to write.

For some reason the way this site is laid out make me scroll idly up and down the page, wishing I could have written more, knowing ironically that the next post I make will automatically bump the last one off the list. But that's OK--it's at just about the right length as it is. Somehow I've come up with a design that hits, for me, that difficult little balance that says "this is really cool, I want more."

It's so easy to have *too much* on one page. Having *just enough* is hard to do, but I think I've actually managed to do that. Go me.
