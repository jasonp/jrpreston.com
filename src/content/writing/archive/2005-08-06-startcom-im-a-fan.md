I just thought I'd reiterate my praise for the newfound home page at [Start.com](http://www.start.com/3) by relaying a two-day e-mail turnaround via their "feedback" link:

>   
> From: Jason Preston \[mailto:\]  
> Sent: Wednesday, August 03, 2005 6:04 PM  
> To: Start.com Feedback  
> Subject: myw3b feedback
> 
> You should have an option to add a "bookmarks" section (widget?) to the page -- Google offers this now on their customizeable home, and it's useful for links that I use frequently, but not frequently enough to warrant space on my linkbar.
> 
> \- Jason

And within two days:

> Hi Jason,  
>   
> Actually we are in the process of testing out our bookmarks widget as we speak and hope to have it on the site in the next few days. Look for it to show up under Staff Picks called "Favorites". You'll be able to add bookmarks one-by-one or import them from a bookmark site via OPML. Shortly afterwards we'll also have a way to import them directly from your IE favorites.  
>   
> Thanks for the feedback!  
> Steve

I just feel cool because he wrote back.
