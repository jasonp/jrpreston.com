Just got back from watching the midnight showing of Pirates 2 - I'm pretty tired so I'll keep the thoughts brief.

It was a lot better than I expected it to be, and for the most part, as good as the first movie. There was a little too much nostalgia and a few scenes that went just a bit too much over the top, but it's definitely a solid picture.

Also, you should know before going in that the movie does not end - it leads into the "second part" - [due out sometime in 2007-ish](http://imdb.com/title/tt0449088/).
