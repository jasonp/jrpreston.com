It's strange to me how some thoughts are really condusive to blogging and others aren't.

A lot of the time I think that if I just kept a running tab of my neverending stream of consciousness, I could have a zillion interesting posts per day, but then I realize that I sometimes I go for days where I'm either too busy to make a thoughtful post, or none of my thoughts would really make good blog posts.

For example, if I'm wandering around a mall and I think to myself "How long does it take Auntie Anne's to make a pretzel?" it's probably not good blogging material.

I go whole days on thoughts like that. It's incredible. I think that's why I made a "nonsense" category.
