Took the drive with my family down to Ashland today to see a few shows at the [Oregon Shakespeare Festival](http://www.orshakes.org/). We just arrived at the motel which sports a fancy Wi-Fi network (hooray modern technology), and it is *hot* here. On the drive down the thermometer in the car read over 100 degrees...which wouldn't be so bad if there weren't a dose of humidity to go with it.

\-------

Certain movies make me want to act. On the ride down we watched *America's Sweethearts*, which is one of those movies. Come to think of it, pretty much any movie featuring John Cusak makes me want to resurrect my fairly defunct acting career on the off chance that someone might decide I'd play a good lead in a romantic comedy.

So, uh...if any movie producers are reading this...e-mail me for my headshot :)
