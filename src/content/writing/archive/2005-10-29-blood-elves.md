Blizzard has announced the official expansion to WoW (that everyone knew was going to happen anyway), but they also gave Gamespot a [look at the new Blood Elves](http://www.gamespot.com/pc/rpg/worldofwarcraftexp1/news.html?sid=6136860). This race was tagged on along purportedly to help balance the uncanny tendency for people to choose Alliance characters rather than Horde characters.

My question is this, though. Who is planning to buy the expansion and *not* create a Blood Elf?
