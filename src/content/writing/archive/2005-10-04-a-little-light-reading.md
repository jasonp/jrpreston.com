I just purchased [Nonfiction](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/redirect?link_code=ur2&camp=1789&tag=flickergaming-20&creative=9325&path=tg/detail/-/0224063022/qid=1128448353/sr=1-1/ref=sr_1_1?v=glance%26s=books)[Image removed], by [Image removed]Chuck Palahniuk at the student bookstore here. I went in looking for some Klosterman, but I can't complain too much.

Especially since, now that I look, it appears to be [over $50 on Amazon](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/redirect?link_code=ur2&camp=1789&tag=flickergaming-20&creative=9325&path=tg/detail/-/0224063022/qid=1128448353/sr=1-1/ref=sr_1_1?v=glance%26s=books)[Image removed].

In any case, it's a collection of nonfiction essays that Palahniuk wrote over the past several years throughout his...experiences...writing his now-famous books. I've read the first three essays, and they're excellent. I'll let you know how the book is when I'm done.
