I've been putting up with a lot of problems from BlueHost recently. I didn't bother to link to them because their entire system has gone down for the second time today.

If things don't settle out reasonably soon, I'll start giving serious thought to abandoning the full deposit I paid and flipping hosts to something more reliable.

It's hard enough to try and build a blogging business with inbound links and buzz and traffic from scratch, but it's damn near impossible to do if our site is never up.
