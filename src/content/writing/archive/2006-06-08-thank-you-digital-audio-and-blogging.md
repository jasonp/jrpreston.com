I feel like I just experienced "web 2.0."

While reading my friend Ben's [blog](http://www.adlin.com), I clicked through his blogroll to Mark Boulton's [blog](http://www.markboulton.co.uk/journal), where I found a link to an [SXSW media page](http://2006.sxsw.com/coverage/podcasts/), where I found a bunch of cool looking "interactive panel podcasts."

Sounds like a sweet conference. Despite the fact that I've worked on [Blog Business Summit](http://www.blogbusinesssummit.com) conferences before, I've never had the chance to attend any of these things, and it's probably something I would enjoy.

Anyways, aside from Boulton's panel, I found some other recordings that given my [recent](http://www.jason-preston.com/index.php/2006/06/06/seo-and-getting-web-traffic/) [obsession](http://www.jason-preston.com/index.php/2006/06/01/its-a-good-faith-world-online/) with [starting](http://www.jason-preston.com/index.php/2006/05/15/social-tagging-is-my-friend/) a [web](http://www.jason-preston.com/index.php/2006/05/14/the-blog-bounce/) [company](http://www.jason-preston.com/index.php/2006/05/10/launch-day/), I look forward to listening to:  

  
-   Building Buzz for Your Web Project \*ding\*
  
-   Sink or Swim: The Five Most Important Startup Decisions
  
-   Running Your New Media Business
  

  
Undoubtedly, some of them will turn out to be less interesting than others, but the titles do tantalize!

Maybe I should make a category for business/startup related posts.
