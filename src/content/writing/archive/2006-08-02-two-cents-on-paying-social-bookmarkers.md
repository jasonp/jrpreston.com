[Image: digg lego logo]I never commented [on this](http://www.calacanis.com/2006/07/18/everyones-gotta-eat-or-1-000-a-month-for-doing-what-youre/) because I thought it would die out pretty quickly, but the blogosphere is all about beating a dead horse, so I'm gonna drag the carcass in here for two quick cents:  

  
2.  [New Netscape](http://www.netscape.com) has always been centered around mixing editorial content with community content. How can paying people to, like an editor, select stories for "print" *possibly* be an unexpected or unplanned or even unreasonable extension of the concept?
  
4.  If paying someone to do more of something they do well invalidates that work, then the whole basis for our capitalistic society falls apart. Why are we paying athletes? So Jason spotted people doing something that, if doing it paid the bills, they could do *even better*? Gee, what a horrible idea it is to pay them.
