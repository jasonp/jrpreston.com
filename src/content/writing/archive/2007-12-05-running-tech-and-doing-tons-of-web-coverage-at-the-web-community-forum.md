[Image: wcf speaking]Today is day one of the [Web Community Forum](http://www.webcommunityforum.com) - you know, that Facebook conference that I've been working on for the past few months. It's been a great chance to meet very cool new people and to catch up with people I haven't seen in a while.

I've been talking with Jason Beckerman and [Mike Germano](http://carrotblog.com/), [Tris Hussey](http://ca.blognation.com/), [Jeremiah Owyang](http://www.web-strategist.com) (one of my absolute favorites in the social media space), had a chance to meet [Connie Bensen](http://www.conniebensen.com/), [Ellen Petry Leanse](http://socialpoweradvisors.com/), [Dean Browell](http://iskew.livejournal.com/), and Lee Lorenzen.

I've also been tweeting quotes and tidbits from the panels all day today, you can find those over @[webcomforum](http://www.twitter.com/webcomforum). Eventually I'll be posting the pictures I've snapped to [my FlickR account](http://flickr.com/photos/jasonp107/), so you'll be able to see those as well. I've asked other people to tag all their pictures on FlickR with wcf07 so you can catch them there.

I'd also expect some pictures to turn up on Facebook. This is a Facebook event, after all.
