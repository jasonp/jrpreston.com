Looking through reviews on the projectors has been a bit of an experience. What I would really like to do, obviously, is have a look at the picture before I drop a significant pile of cash on one. However, it doesn't look like that's going to be a possibility.

I've been looking at getting the reasonably price BenQ PE7800, and I've seen a number of reviews for the 7700 (but I haven't been able to find a way to \*buy\* said 7700 until today). Anyways, the 7700 is about an extra thousand dollars, so apparently it's a better model.

Only now have I found a review for the actual product. I've never seen something so difficult to track down reviews on. Still they get [good ratings](http://www.projectorcentral.com/BenQ-PE7800.htm).

I'm severely tempted.
