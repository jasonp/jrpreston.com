BARCELONA - A rainy, friday afternoon saw my six traveling companions and I wading our way through the busy streets and sidewalks on multicolored bikes.

In all seriousness, we took an incredibly cool bike tour around the city, hitting up a couple of the more important sites and a couple of the more out-of-the-way but neat looking pubs. I really wish I had a way to upload pictures from this computer, but I can't think of a way to cleverly move pictures from my camera to the public computer in the hostel.

Suffice it to say, Barcelona is a beautiful city, and even though it rained a fair amount of the afternoon, it's been an incredibly fun vacation so far.

It's also legal to be naked anywhere in the city, at any time.

Pictures when I get back!
