I [posted before](http://www.jason-preston.com/index.php/2006/03/13/google-calendar/) about how Google was working through the kinds on creating a calendar setup to work in conjunction with gmail and be all Ajax and flashy.

It looks as though the calendar got ready a lot faster than people thought. Just for fun I entered calendar.google.com into my address bar, only to find it up and running.

So I'm going to start using Google Calendar. Since I split my time between a desktop and a laptop it makes a lot of sense for me to use a web-based calendar anyway.
