After reading another one of Evslin's posts about [ebay, skypke, and VOIP](http://blog.tomevslin.com/2005/09/will_ebay_be_yo.html), I'm quite ready to believe that VOIP combined with EVDO (internet via. cell towers) will basically lead (quickly, I hope) to Skype-phones.

I want my next phone to be an mp3-playing, video and still taking, software-running Skype phone. I want a mobile that will connect me to skype users (either on cell phones or computers) for free (minutes...clearly they'll charge monthly for service).

Gimme.
