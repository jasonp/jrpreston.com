For me, using a laptop is like useing a satellite computer--it's got the stuff I need for the moment, and it's good enough to get through the day, but in the end all my stuff just needs to be re-attached to a desktop. There's something solid about it. I don't think I could ever use a laptop as my only computer.

This is particularly true for games. Having an actual monitor, keyboard, and mouse just makes the gameplay experience that much more...grounded. It feels more organized, more in control.

I miss my desktop.
