I'm not very big on webcomics. This is strange considering that I've tried to start one at least three times, although I usually get stuck at the point I realize I can't draw. It's a lot harder than it looks.

Regardless, it's an area that pretty much escapes me most of the time, since I'm really pretty horrible about following anything that comes in an episodic format. It's just a character flaw, I dunno.

But my friend Ben pointed me to [Questionable Content](http://www.questionablecontent.net/) last night, and it's excellent. I'd recommend starting from the beginning, since it all makes a cohesive story. But I dig it.

I'll add it to my list of one other comic (Penny Arcade, if you're curious).
