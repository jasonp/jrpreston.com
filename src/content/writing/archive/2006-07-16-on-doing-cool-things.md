[There's a girl](http://avc.blogs.com/a_vc/2006/07/how_to_be_a_fil.html) who's doing big things by releasing her videos on YouTube.  
  
  
This is a damn good idea, but it won't work for everyone. By definition  
you only hear about the success stories (in terms of The Long Tail:  
hits), so it's a skewed picture.  
  
This kind of thing always makes me conflicted, but I think it's because deep down I must be a horrible person. I'm happy that it's possible and I'm happy that people are doing cool things like this, but at the same time I always get upset because it's not me doing them.  
  
I also get frustrated that nobody thinks what I'm doing is as cool as these things ;)
