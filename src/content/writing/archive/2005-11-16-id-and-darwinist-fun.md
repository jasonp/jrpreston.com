Scott Adams [wrote a post](http://dilbertblog.typepad.com/the_dilbert_blog/2005/11/intelligent_des.html) several days ago about how he was doing research on Intelligent Design and Darwinism in order to find good joke material. I have no doubt he found plenty.

What's most interesting is how [some guy responded to him](http://pharyngula.org/index/weblog/comments/scott_adams_is_a_wally/).

And how [Adams wrote back](http://dilbertblog.typepad.com/the_dilbert_blog/2005/11/intelligent_des_1.html).

The main point of Adams' first post was how he found out, incredibly, that everyone was tied up defacing "those other bastards," and nobody really knew anything about what they were arguing.

If I were Scott, my response to Mr. Pharyngula (aka "some guy who responded) would be: where's your evidence? Take a read through, and you'll find that the post consists of:  

  
2.  How Scott Adams is an idiot
  
4.  There's plenty of evidence, but I'm not going to tell you

Anyhoo - I really *really* wish Douglas Adams was alive right now. You can bet he'd have a blog. And you can bet it'd be an even better read than this one.
