Ever since [eBay purchased skype](http://www.americasnetwork.com/americasnetwork/article/articleDetail.jsp?id=181757) for an amazing $2.6 billion (good job Skype team), the question has been flying around the blogosphere: What the hell was eBay thinking?

It's a good question. Espeically considering i've seen it debated on [A VC's journal](http://avc.blogs.com/a_vc/2005/09/ebay_and_skype.html).

(Edit: Fred added [a post today](http://avc.blogs.com/a_vc/2005/09/ebay_and_skype__1.html) about the same topic.)

But I saw an explanation today that makes perfect sense to me, left in the comments at [Bubblegeneration](http://www.bubblegeneration.com/2005/09/web-2.cfm):

> Back to eBay. With the purchase of Skype, eBay will create a marketplace for professional services; listed on eBay at a $/min basis, fulfilled via skype.
> 
> When this happens: A "service listing" on eBay, unlike today's "product listing" becomes a re-usable microchunk from the perspective of the seller! For this reason, eBay + Skype has the potential to start creating cumulative value once eBay adds professional services to their Marketplace. - David Gibbons

If you ignore the econ mumbo-jumbo, it sounds like ebay wants to allow people to sell real-time, VOIP services. Good lord, that's an excellent idea.
