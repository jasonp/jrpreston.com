About 4pm yesterday I decided that there's no way I want to run around all day every day using the touchpad on this laptop. Either I'm just not wired to use one, or I'm too lazy to break myself of my habits.

So I googled up a sweet (and cheap) [wireless mouse](http://www.bestbuy.com/site/olspage.jsp?skuId=7338678&ref=39&loc=01&type=product&id=1122652230276) from MS that tucks the USB part back into the mouse when it's not in use. I ordered it from ProVantage though, not from BestBuy.
