One of the reasons I like World of Warcraft better than anything Sony Online Entertainment has put out on the market is that Blizzard is [actually committed](http://forums.worldofwarcraft.com/thread.aspx?fn=wow-general&t=7874451&p=1&tmp=1#post7874451) to enforcing their terms of use. In other words, they make sure to crack down on cheaters who can ruin the game experience for everyone else.

Sony, on the other hand, thought it was a good idea to open their own [online marketplace](http://stationexchange.station.sony.com/) so they could simply cash in on the cheating.

I'm pretty sure this is like drugs. Blizzard continues to fight the drugs, and Sony decided to make everything legal and just tax it.

Pick your worlds. I prefer Blizzard.
