I think it's kind of cool how Facebook sends out an e-mail when you get a new friend invite, someone writes on your wall, or you get invited to an event.  
  
I don't usually check the Facebook site very regularly, so otherwise I'd probably miss basically every time that someone tries to interact with me. But it's kind of silly the way I always get event invitation e-mails *after* they happen.  
  
I just got an invite this morning to a party yesterday night. I think I'll go.
