What if there were a device that combined the features and looks of both the iPhone and the Kindle? Something that was about the size of a magazine, but had a touch screen interface?

What if you could get one for free, and for $10 a year you could get your favorite magazine delivered to it digitally?

I wonder if that's a workable business model. I would use it.
