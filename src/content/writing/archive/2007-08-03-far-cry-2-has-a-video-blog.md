The Far Cry 2 dev team is smart to have a blog. They're smarter to post the link in the [Evil Avatar](http://www.evilavatar.com/forums/showthread.php?p=902750#post902750) forums, which I am known to haunt on occasion:

> Like our producer Louis-Pierre Pharand says on the intro page, the purpose of the video blogs is to show you guys all the cool things the team is doing, whether it's in a video, an audio clip or pictures, but keep in mind that it won't be game information all the time. We also want to show you guys the people behind the game how it all comes together it the end to make a kick-ass game

I give the team props for going about the game-o-sphere and engaging.

You can check out their first videoblog post which I've embedded after the jump

I have to add, though, that it's a little unclassy to post in a forum and then ask for people to leave comments and feedback on your own:

> BTW - If you REALLY have SUGGESTIONS and COMMENTS, DO post them in the BLOG'S COMMENTS or on our FarCry 2 FORUM (and say 'hi' to Kimi while you're at it ). We really do read those and we REALLY appreciate your feedback.

tsk tsk.
