[Image: samus]I can't say that I'm a really big Metroid Prime fan, but I do find this amusing.

From the press release:

> This version is based on the Zero Suit seen at the end of Metroid Prime 2 Echoes. First 4 Figures has used the official game files in order to create an extremely accurate recreation of the Zero Suit. She comes armed with her signature pistol and has curves in all the right places!

Curves in all the right places? No way!

This thing is about 9" tall and apparently costs just over $100. Not something to add to my shelf, but then again I have a giant Yoda taking up all the space, so I'm not one to talk. If this is your bag, [go nuts](http://www.first4figures.com/product_details.asp?range=23&id=49).
