1\. For a long time I've made the habit of checking the [WIRED News](http://www.wired.com/) page for cool and interesting stories, and it was one of the first things I added to my start page. Unfortunately, in the past month or so they must have been bought by Lycos, because there's an *amazingly* ugly Lycos bar on the top of the page which pisses me off to no end, and looks horrible to boot.

  

And although I applaud Wired for embracing blogs as the publishing tools they are, the front page of Wired News is where I want to find your daily or weekly columnists' offerings, not your blog entries. It used to be I'd find a few worthwhile articles every day, and now they're merely posts that rotate far too quickly to be worthwile.

  

2\. The April issue of [WIRED](http://www.wired.com/wired/) (the mag), which I may go pick up on the stands, is guest-edited by [Will Wright](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Wright), and all about games. Way cool.
