[Image: Intellitext]Does anyone else think that this IntelliTXT style advertising that keeps showing up (on a lot of gaming sites, incidentally) is extremely annoying? The last thing I want to see while I'm scrolling through an article is for an advertisement bubble to pop up over what I'm reading.

One of the keys to successful advertising is *not being annoying*.  
This is true for the product being advertised, and it's true for the site the advertising is on. I'm going to steal a quote I [saw recently](http://www.veen.com/jeff/archives/000901.html) to underline this:

> To predict the behavior of ordinary people in advance, you only have to assume that they will always try to escape a disagreeable situation with the smallest possible expenditure of intelligence.
> 
> *\- [Friedrich Nietzsche](http://history.enotes.com/famous-quotes/to-predict-the-behavior-of-ordinary-people-in)*

If your site is annoying because of your ads, I'm going to visit it less often (unfortunately true for the Dilbert Blog, one of my favorites). I'm simply trying to escape a disagreeable situation.
