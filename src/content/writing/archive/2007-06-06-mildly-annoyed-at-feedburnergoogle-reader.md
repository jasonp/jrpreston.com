When I use Google Reader (always), I like to right-click and grab URLs of posts I want to link to, but when I grab posts from, say, Fred Wilson, it gives me the feedburner link.

It still gets the reader there, but I want to give Fred some link love, and I want him to notice I linked to him, both of which are less likely if I'm linking to feedburner. So I have to click through to the post and then copy the URL, which is lame.
