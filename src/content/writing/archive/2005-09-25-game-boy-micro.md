Engadget has a [bunch of juicy pictures](http://gaming.engadget.com/entry/1234000917060290/) of the new Game Boy Micro being unpacked (they got one before everyone else does, I guess).

I've actually always been a fan of Nintendo, and despite the fact that they have a history of coming up with [weirdass controllers](http://buttonmashing.com/2005/09/15/tokyo-game-show/), they've managed to hold on with the aid of their ridiculously good catalogue of franchise games.

I hope this next round goes well for them.
