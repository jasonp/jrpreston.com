Way back when I first picked up my MacBookPro, I scoured the net for cool downloadables, and drew up my own list of [10 essential apps for the new mac user](http://www.jason-preston.com/index.php/2007/01/20/10-essential-apps-for-the-new-mac-user/). Today I spotted a list of [5 free apps for bloggers](http://www.applegazette.com/software/5-free-os-x-apps-every-blogger-should-have/), which linked to an app called [iGTD](http://bargiel.home.pl/iGTD/).

I have no idea what the "Getting Things Done" method is, but I'm am frequently, hopelessly disorganized. I live my life by stickies.

If you want me to do something really bizarre, all you have to do is paste it into one of my "To Do Today" Stickies and I'll find myself on an airplane to Zimbabwe with six thousands dollars cash before I realize anything odd has happened.

In any case, I'm going to download this thing and give it a whirl. Maybe it'll help me out.

UPDATE: This looks far too complicated for me. I checked the [Wikipedia article](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Getting_Things_Done) and some stranger with no credibility confirms my suspicions.

Now if you'll excuse me, there's apparently some business in Zimbabwe I need to take care of.
