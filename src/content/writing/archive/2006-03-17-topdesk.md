[Image removed]There's a great utility called [TopDesk](http://www.otakusoftware.com/topdesk/index.html) for Windows XP that offers some expose-like functionality to my workspace.

I've been trying it out for the full trial period, and I really like it. I have a post coming up where I give it (and other mac-like PC app) a full review, but right now I have to figure out how I'm going to keep using it.

I think ten bucks is perfectly reasonable for using TopDesk. I'm glad to support their work. But I also think that $20 is too much just to use it on both my laptop and my desktop. I should be able to install it on both for $10.

Sounds good to me. But despite the fact that Otaku Software keeps a [blog](http://www.otakusoftware.com/blog/), they haven't found time to respond to my e-mail (sent on Monday) asking whether or not I need to buy two licenses just to use it on both of my computers.

I don't want to buy one and then have to buy two. And I don't want to buy two and need only one. So I haven't bought it yet.

But keep an eye out for my full thoughts on the app.
