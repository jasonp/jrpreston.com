-   When you go to a restaurant, you (usually)pay at the end of the meal, after you have been served
  
-   People get out of the way for an ambulance, because saving a life is worth being late to your meeting
  
-   You can call 911 from a cell phone with no sim card
  
-   If you ask for a free cup of water in a business that has a running tap, you will almost always get one
  
-   There is no tax on unprepared food
  

I think it's possible that people are fundamentally good.
