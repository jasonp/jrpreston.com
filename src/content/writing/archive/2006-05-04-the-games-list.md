This list comes up often enough in my head that I figure I should go ahead and put it down. Maybe someday I'll add it to the sidebar with my other lists.

**The list of games I have beaten**. (Or arguably "completed").

Despite the fact that I've played gazillions of games, especially on the PC, there's a relatively small number of games I've ever actually played all the way through (without just /nocliping to the end). I think the number is somewhere under 5%, and to me it means that there was something so phenominal about the game that it captured me all the way through.

So here's that list, in no particular order:  

  
-   TIE Fighter
  
-   Half-Life (one, two, espisode one)
  
-   Diablo II (but not the first one)
  
-   Final Fantasy VII
  
-   Deus Ex
  
-   Prince of Persia: The Sands of Time
  
-   Donkey Kong Country
  
-   World of Warcraft (hit 60 before the expansion)
  
-   Counter-Strike (OK I know you can't \*beat\* this, but I still play it, so I think that counts)
  
-   God of War II

That's it. Short list, huh?
