For all that I am a fan of consolidating things in my life (not too much in my pockets, use my phone as an alarm clock...) I'm horribly, horribly bad at consolidating my online life.

I have far too many e-mail addresses, as I've said before, and I can't really make myself get rid of them or move everything over to one place. By the same token, I'm realizing just how *much* writing and "webing" I'm doing.

I've got this blog here, plus the [Flicker](http://www.flickergaming.net) e-zine to edit and run, plus I'm blogging at [Play Everything](http://www.playeverything.net), and I'm really, sorely tempted to finally start putting up a webcomic I started work on with a friend over a year ago.

But how much is too much? Sometimes wonder if I'll be able to write after the age of thirty.
