In somewhat delayed response to Alex Payne's [objection to "everything buckets,"](http://al3x.net/2009/01/31/against-everything-buckets.html) i.e. software like [Yojimbo](http://www.barebones.com/products/Yojimbo/), I'd like to make the following comment:

Yes, computers work better with structured data.

I do not want to structure my data.

I want my computer to structure my data for me.

This is the definition of an everything bucket: a program that structures my data for me.
