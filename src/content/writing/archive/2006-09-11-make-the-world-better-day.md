[Image removed]To a large extent I feel like all the movies and media coverage about september 11th, especially since we're...let's see...five years down the road, belittles the event and the people who died and the people who did heroic things to make sure fewer people died.

So I tend to want to ignore the September 11th hullabaloo, but [this is a pretty good idea](http://5tephe.livejournal.com/47777.html):

> Make today not about the death, and destruction, and violence, and intolerance, but about the gift that you can make, yourself, right now.
> 
> Go out today and do something tangible, that makes the world better.
> 
> Visit someone in hospital, give blood, make a $5 donation to a charity, bake a cake for a neighbour, fix up that hole in your mother-in-law's fence...

So I'm down. Who else?
