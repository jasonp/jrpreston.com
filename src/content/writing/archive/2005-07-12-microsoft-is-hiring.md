There have been rumors throughout the past several months (starting with a Wall Street Journal article I can't find) about "corporate blogger" becomimg more and more a mainstream job title. What started for millions of people as a personal hobby is beginning to blossom into an actual Job market.

[Online Media Daily](http://publications.mediapost.com/) notes that Microsoft has [begun to post](http://publications.mediapost.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=Articles.showArticleHomePage&art_aid=31467) several blogging jobs through MSN. The article says that:

"MICROSOFT'S MSN RECENTLY PUT OUT a call for paid bloggers to write and edit Web sites about five broad topics: fashion/food/style, music, sports, technology, and television..."

So..get crackin!
