[Image: doom.png]I know that sometimes it might seem like I sit here and regurgitate Valve press releases, but the reality of it is that I only regurgitate the awesome ones, like this one:

> DOOM, HeXen, QUAKE, and More Legendary Titles Launch Today
> 
> QUAKECON 2007-MESQUITE, TX-AUGUST 3, 2007-id Software has teamed with Valve to bring some of the most successful game franchises in history to Steam, a leading platform for the delivery and management of PC games and digital content with over 13 million accounts around the world.
> 
> Starting today, Steam users may download full versions of these best-selling and critically-acclaimed games, including the complete DOOM series and QUAKE I - III, including mission packs, as well as id's pioneering PC side-scroller, Commander Keen and vintage favorites Wolfenstein 3D, Spear of Destiny, Heretic, and HeXen. As part of the id Welcome Celebration on Steam, select id packages are being offered with a 10%, limited-time discount.

It's looking more and more like Steam is going to become the Wii Virtual Console of the PC, carrying all my favorite games from [Deus Ex](http://steampowered.com/v/index.php?area=game&AppId=6910&cc=US) (if you haven't yet played it, you should do so immediately) to [QUAKE](http://steampowered.com/v/index.php?area=game&AppId=2310&cc=US). Excuse me while I go cry with happiness.
