You can no longer find NBC clips all over YouTube, since after all, they're copyrighted video and it's a violation to put them up in the first place.

However, the most popular videos can be found on NBC's own site [here](http://www.nbc.com/Video/).

It's the right idea, and I'm glad NBC is moving in the right direction...but I want to see a much bigger library and call me picky but I also want the videos to play more than halfway through. (Anyone else having that problem?)

If these things get ironed out, NBC could be first on the 'net clip bandwagon.
