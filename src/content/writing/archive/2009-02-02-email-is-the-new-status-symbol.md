So says the [New York Times](http://www.nytimes.com/2009/02/01/us/politics/01obama.html?_r=2&hp=&pagewanted=all). My favorite part:

> Mr. Gates does not use e-mail for work, said Geoff Morrell, the Pentagon press secretary, and besides, he sees the president often enough that he does not need it to communicate with him by e-mail.

I send e-mail to people that I see every single day. It's a weird idea to me that someone thinks they could "not need" to send e-mail to the President.
