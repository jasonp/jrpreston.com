[Image: BlogTips Pic]Just yesterday we Blog Business Summit folks launched our little [BlogTips Facebook Application](http://www.facebook.com/apps/application.php?id=2465293443), which, although the development hasn't been very hands-on for me, has been fun to have going. It's also fun to watch people sign up - we're up to 41 members already, which is excellent news.

"What does it do?" What a silly question - it provides blogging tips! The cool part is that the tips are all user-submitted. Add the application and submit as many blogging tips as you like, and every day we select a new tip to be displayed on the application widget.

And just for fun, because we like people, before the conference we're having our speakers review all the submitted tips to pick the best one, and whoever submitted that tip gets a free 3-day pass to the Chicago BBS this September.

Think you got the blogging chops? Then go win a free conference pass.
