---
title: "What Claude gets wrong about office hours"
description: "The forcing question is the product. The friendly preamble is the failure mode."
pubDate: 2026-04-08
tags: [ai, founders]
---

Office hours are a forcing function. The whole point is the question you don't want to answer — *what's the smallest version of this that you could ship by Friday?* — landing in a moment when there's no good way out except to commit to something.

When you ask an AI to play office hours, it gets the structure right and the temperature wrong. It welcomes you. It asks how you're doing. It softens the forcing question into a request. It is trying to be helpful, and the helpfulness is the bug.

The fix isn't to make the AI rude. It's to make the AI confident enough that the question lands as it would land from someone who has heard a thousand pitches and is doing you a kindness by not pretending you have more time than you do.
