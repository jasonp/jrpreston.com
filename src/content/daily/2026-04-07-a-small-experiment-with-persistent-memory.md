title: "A small experiment with persistent memory"
description: "What happens when the assistant remembers what you didn't think to write down."
pubDate: 2026-04-07
tags: [ai, memory]
---

I've been running a small experiment with persistent memory in my AI workflow. The rule I gave myself: only save things the next session would have no other way to know.

A week in, the surprising part isn't what got saved. It's what didn't. Most of the things I thought were worth remembering turned out to be reconstructable from the code, the commit history, or one quick re-read of the relevant file. The actual keepers are about *me* — preferences, taste calls, the reasons I made choices that look arbitrary from the outside.

The assistant doesn't need a longer memory of my codebase. It needs a clearer picture of how I think about it. Those are different problems.
