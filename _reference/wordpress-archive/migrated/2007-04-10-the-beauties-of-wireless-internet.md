I'm hanging out at my friend Jeff's apartment, a whole twenty feet away from mine, and I'm still logged in to my own internet connection.

Honestly I think it's pretty awesome that I have a good friend who lives close enough that I'm sitting in his living room and using my internet. Actually I just think it's pretty cool that I'm sitting in someone else's living room and using my internet. OK I'm a geek.
