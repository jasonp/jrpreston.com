[![](http://static.flickr.com/62/212131390_891e39a4df_m.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photo_zoom.gne?id=212131390&size=s)[Some people](http://waltercrespo.blogspot.com/) are figuring out that tagging things in our Linkbox is a great way to draw some attention to themselves and drive a little traffic their way.

Like all social tagging services, our Linkbox lets anyone have a real voice on OUR FRONT PAGE. Smart site owners know that letting someone else do the work to build up an audience and then basically advertising relevant content for free can really pay off.

Sometimes we even pull [main page posts](http://www.flickergaming.net/index.php/2006/08/11/bully-trailer/) from what gets seeded there.

So do yourself a favor and give the linkbox a whirl.
