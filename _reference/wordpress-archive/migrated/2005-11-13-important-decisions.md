After getting back from Barcelona, I poked around the Fossil site for far too long, and narrowed my choices down to two wallets. Now...which one should I get? (click pictures to see official description).

**Minerva Tri Fold** - $40  
This is the classic Fossil tri fold wallet, essentially the same was the one I used to have. Good for nostalgia, good for looking slick, and just a sweet wallet overall.  
  

[![](http://www.fossil.com/images/us/local/products/detail/ML2252001.jpg)](http://www.fossil.com/shopping/product/detailmain.jsp?itemID=27406&itemType=PRODUCT&iMainCat=983&iSubCat=989&iProductID=27406&selectedcolor=1836)

**Aviator Front Pocket Bi Fold** - $20  
This would be the first bi fold for me, and if you look carefully you'll see that there's no actual "walletness" to it, just a bunch of card slides. This is because it has a money clip instead, which is really cool. The only problem is there's no way to confirm that this is, in fact, a metal wire inside the fold, because a clip on the outside would not actually be very cool, and I wouldn't really want this one at all.
