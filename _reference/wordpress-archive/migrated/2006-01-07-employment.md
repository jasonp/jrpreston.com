Against all kinds of odds, I've actually managed to get myself back into the coolest job I've ever had.

Starting essentially yesterday, I'm once again working for the folks who run the Blog Business Summit in various capacities. While I'm around town, it'll be actual employment. While I'm out of town, I'll probably spend most of my time posting to [RawFormat](http://www.rawformat.com), the blog that covers, well, Raw format issues.

It should be really interesting stuff, and I'm looking forward to it. I've been thinking of keeping an aggregate of all my posts here, so I may start sticking links to my other writings up as asides. We'll see how it goes.
