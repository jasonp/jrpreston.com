[![Spencer and I](http://static.flickr.com/29/101392411_600a94e813_m.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/jasonp107/101392411/ "Photo Sharing")Thought I'd make a quick post since we're having a bunch of fun up here at Whistler. Spencer and I got up a little late since the lifts are basically only open until 3:30, but I imagine we'll try to make it to the slopes a little earlier tomorrow.

The snow is incredibly nice and it's clear out, although yesterday it was down to -50 degrees F at the top, and we didn't try to go up there today since winds were still reported at around 20 mph. It was very tempting, though.
