I've been so busy recently with everything I'm trying to do. I made a list last night of all the things I'd like to get done "in the near future" and it's literally two pages long. I've got about 5-10 different essay-length entries I'd like to find time to write up, and three different short ideas in the queue. Probably the most interesting one is going to be my version of "what makes a good blogger, and why I'm not," but who knows when I'll get to that.

This summer has been going amazingly fast. I've been out of school for almost two months, but it hardly feels like it. I'm working 40 hours a week on a really cool job, but if you ask me, I can't really tell you what it is that I do. Probably because I'm not really sure, either. By the time I'm done working, I usually have some sort of plan for the evening that involves just hanging out with my friends, some of whom (David) I won't see from this Saturday until next January. (!!!!!)

My family all signed up for Hunter Education Class, which has turned out to be more of a gun show than any sort of instructional class so far, but I'll write about that later, when I have time to (ha!). I've got that class again tonight from 6 to 9:30, and tomorrow at 8am. I don't know how I'll get up.

The short version? My mind is spinning with so many things it's ridiculous. I need a vacation.
