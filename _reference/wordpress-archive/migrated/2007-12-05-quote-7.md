When the web and all the people we know can start being an audio experience in our living rooms, it's a big deal. I realize that not many people have setups like this, but it shouldn't be too long before it's much more common. A mac mini costs $600. And it puts the web into your living room. Give it a try. I bet you'll like it as much as we do.

From [Fred Wilson](http://avc.blogs.com/a_vc/2007/12/bringing-the-we.html).

He is talking about his media setup - three entertainment systems each with a Mac Mini hooked up to the TV, and how it lets him share video and music throughout the house.

I use a custom-built media center in my house, which for me works in much the same way, and lets me stream recorded TV shows to the computer in my room. Sooner or later, though, computers and cable boxes are going to merge. It's inevitable.
