The gaming e-zine I manage and edit (I guess I'm the editor-in-cheif?) is delivering its second issue tomorrow. Flicker is turning into some good stuff, I'm pretty happy with it. It's a collection of gamers writing collectively about their opinions, doing interviews, and thinking about various game-related issues.

You can find the first issue online [here](http://www.flickergaming.net/blog/?page_id=8).

The second issue is in essentially the same format, but with new content all over the place. It should be really cool, and if you like what you see in the first issue, or you have any interest in gaming, you should go ahead and [sign up](http://www.flickergaming.net/lists/?p=subscribe&id=1) to the e-zine.

Yes, we need your e-mail address, and no, we will never give it to anyone. I will personally rip the throats out of anyone who uses our system to spam you.
