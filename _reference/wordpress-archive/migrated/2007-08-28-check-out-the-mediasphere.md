Today my lovely co-worker [Teresa](http://www.teresacentric.com) was [the guest](http://webcommunityforum.com/2007/08/ill-be-talking-about-facebook-on-the-mediasphere-at-noon-pst-today/) on [The Mediasphere](http://www.blogtalkradio.com/hostpage.aspx?show_id=46438), a cool new show from [Tris Hussey](http://www.larixconsulting.com/) and [Jim Turner](http://www.onebyonemedia.com/), discussing yesterday's announcements.

It's a great episode with a bunch of people calling in, including myself, [Jeremy Pepper](http://pop-pr.blogspot.com/), and Christian ??? (who are you? shoot me an e-mail, I'll ad the link!).

It turned in to a great discussion of Facebook as a business tool, privacy in social networks, and where social media tools are headed in the future. At some point I started waxing poetic about how Facebook is the future "platform" of the web, and I'm pretty sure I also borrowed some ideas from [Fred Wilson](http://avc.blogs.com/a_vc) about finding and adding people in social media services. But that's what happens when you just let me talk.
