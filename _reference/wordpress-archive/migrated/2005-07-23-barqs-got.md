Tomorrow I'll be joining my friends [Ben](http://www.pixelumbrella.com/adlin.com/) and [Rickey](http://www.livejournal.com/users/storyr/) (heads up: he tends to protect his entries) at the Bite of Seattle. They're going to be boothing [Kathy Casey's Alley](http://www.biteofseattle.com/home/featured/#kathy) I gather, which features food from a number of restaurants, including O2, a wonderful little spot in West Seattle they both work for. O2 doesn't have a web site yet, but it's a brand new spinoff of the more established [Ovio Bistro](http://www.oviobistro.com/), and I would highly recommend a visit.

I plan to do some wandering and some biting from noon onwards, and I'll be sure to bring my camera so I should have pictures and a review tomorrow.
