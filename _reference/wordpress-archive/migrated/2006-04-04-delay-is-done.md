According to [Yahoo](http://news.yahoo.com/s/ap/20060404/ap_on_go_co/delay), Tom DeLay has decided he will resign from Congress sometime before June.

Given his track record over the past year or so with his indictment in Texas and the involvement of his former aid in Abramoff's big corruption deal, his resignation shouldn't be a huge surprise.

I'm curious to see where the votes go this November. It'll be an interesting election.
