I like keeping an eye on video game press releases because I get juicy tidbits like these. I've been [salivating](http://www.jason-preston.com/index.php/2005/09/20/hellgate-london/) over Hellgate: London ever since I spotted it in PCGamer last year.

Basically, this game is what happens when you take the majority of the group that worked on Diablo and Diablo II, and you let them make one game for the next five years. It promises to be one hell of a game, meshing up the RPG elements I loved from Diablo with a touch of the FPS feel that always works best for me in games.
