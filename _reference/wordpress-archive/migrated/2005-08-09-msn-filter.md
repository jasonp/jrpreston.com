I'm just going to chalk this up as another idea that I never had a hope of pulling out.

[Flicker](http://www.flickergaming.net) is meant to be a gaming-niche blog. A source of information and opinion about the world of gaming and game development, where readers contributed and a team of bloggers worked together to bring cool content to the site.

In effect, it's my one-man run at doing what [Weblogs Inc.](http://www.weblogsinc.com) and [MSN Filter](http://filter.msn.com/) are doing.

A collection of high-profile, directed blogs. I guess I should go apply to write for one of them ;)
