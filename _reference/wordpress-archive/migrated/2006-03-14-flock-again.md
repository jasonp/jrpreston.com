I mentioned a few days ago that I downloaded Flock to try out the browser.

It's cool and it made a good first impression, but the more I try to use it, the more I get drawn back to Firefox or IE7.

It's a great concept, and the built in tools are fun, but I think it may just be a better idea to make killer extensions for Firefox, because that's basically all it is.

It's also been messing with my del.icio.us account since I gave it the password. It's trying to use del.icio.us for normal bookmarks...I don't think that's what the service was designed for.
