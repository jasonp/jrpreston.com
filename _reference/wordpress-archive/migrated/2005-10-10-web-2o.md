For the web, apparently, the future is now.

The blogosphere is alive with buzz about Web 2.0. Services like [FlickR](http://www.flickr.com) and [del.icio.us](http://del.icio.us) are the first in what is already being called the [bubble 2.0](http://blog.tomevslin.com/2005/10/bubble_20_intro.html) of web development.

In the rush to revolutionize the web, I feel as though things are going to become sloppy again. For a while now, the internet has moved at a fairly cautious pace. As a result, we've seen some incredibly good, solid services and intelligent innovation.

I hope that as companies begin to do whatever they can to join the "web 2.0" world---the nextgen world of internet services, if you will---that people continue to dedicate the time and effort to each service that we've seen over the past few years.
