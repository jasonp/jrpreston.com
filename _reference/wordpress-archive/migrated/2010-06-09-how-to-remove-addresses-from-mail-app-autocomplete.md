[How to remove addresses from Mail.app autocomplete](http://forums.macosxhints.com/archive/index.php/t-17995.html)

> In Mail.app, under the "Window" menu, select: Previous Recipients. A list of all previous recipients will come up. Those that are already in your address book will have the little Rolodex icon next to them. There are two buttons: 1) Remove From List 2) Add to Address Book
