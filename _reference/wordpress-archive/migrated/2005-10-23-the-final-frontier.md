If you can't get Americans to *like* space (and science) anymore, well, how are you going to get them to invest in it? I guess we have to offer [Space Rocket Races and Big Cash Prizes](http://www.wired.com/news/technology/0,1282,69309,00.html?tw=wn_tophead_1) for new stuff.

Of course, I've always liked space. I've also liked the romanticism of having a boat on the ocean, but I get seasick. I wonder if I get spacesick, too?
