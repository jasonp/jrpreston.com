I'm a big fan of Firefox. I think the first time I switched browsers was on my laptop last year in philosophy. I've been known to carry the thing to boring classes and peruse the net instead of paying attention.

Halfway through the semester, I downloaded Firefox from a link I found on some web design site. Next time I opened my browser in class, the guy who sat behind me leaned up and tapped me on the shoulder.

"I'm glad to see you've got Firefox," he whispered.

I glanced at him questioningly, "oh yeah?"

"Yeah. I've been watching you all semester, and every day I wanted to tell you to switch. You'll love this thing."

And he was right...I do. Only in the past week have I really discovered the flexibility I get through Firefox [Extensions](https://addons.mozilla.org/extensions/?application=firefox) and [Themes](https://addons.mozilla.org/themes/?application=firefox/)...I've been sloshing through the lists finding my favorite add-ons for the past few days. I don't have time right this second, but I'll come back later and list the ones I've found that I liked...and my one small gripe about Mozilla.
