I'm on the phone with Pizza Hut trying to order a pizza. The fact that i'm making a blog post *while* I'm on the phone should tell you something is wrong...

It took me about five minutes to give them my phone number, no joke. Now he's trying to figure out that I want to order a pizza. Yes a pizza. No not ten pizzas. Yes a pizza. What size? A Large. No not a medium. OK he hasn't put me on hold, but the phone is squealing and he's helping someone at the counter. This has happened two times before. GOOD GOD.

OK he finally handed the phone to someone else. Wow, that was easy.

The timer on my phone says 7:56 to order a friggin' pizza for take out. That's not even deliver. The guy ignored the phone three times without even telling me I was "on hold," and had me repeat things at least three times. You gotta have the right people answering the phones.

That's really crap service. I'm not even sure why I stayed on the line.
