I haven't gotten ballsy enough to put a sitemeter up on Jason-Preston.com yet. Although I track my statistics obsessively, I feel like there's a minimum embarrassment threshold I have to reach before I can confidently post my traffic. I'll get there someday.

In the meantime, I'm going to share with everyone the top five search terms that have clicked-through to my site in the past week. They are, in order:

1.  Jason Preston
  
3.  firefox 2.0 beta
  
5.  dancing skeletons
  
7.  wow grinding
  
9.  grinding spots

Now, the first one makes a good amount of sense, but the rest of them show a really cool phenomenon:

*blogs are not only temporarily relevant*. A blog is in fact a wonderful repository of opinion, commentary, and information that adds to the value of the internet overall.

I wrote my post about [where to grind in World of Warcraft](http://www.jason-preston.com/index.php/2006/02/13/wow-grinding-guide/) back in early February, and it's still two of the top five search terms for me.

But number 3 is definitely my favorite: [Dancing Skeletons](http://www.jason-preston.com/media/barcelona.wmv).

Sure I've got that here, but honestly, who searches for that? ;)
