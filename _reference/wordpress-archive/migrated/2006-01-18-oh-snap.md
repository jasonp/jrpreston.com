I think part of the reason I enjoy reading blogs so much is because they tend to follow this general formula:  

> I read this post by someone.
> 
> They are an idiot, and this is why. Here's someone else who agrees.

  
For some reason, I really get into this format, especially if the writer is particularly articulate (say that ten times fast). It's some sort of buzz from vicariously experiencing the argument, which is weird.

But isn't it just so satisfying to watch someone get literarily bitchslapped?
