I'm always amazed at the things I can't do in Photoshop.

It's a lot more likely that they *can* be done, and I just don't know my way around the tools well enough to really get things done right, but they seem so damn *simple* that they ought to be easy to find.

For example: the site logo? I rounded the corners on the image today so that it goes better with the new site color (white...). But I couldn't do that in photoshop! I had to go to MS paint, create a rounded-corner non-filled white box (another thing you can't do in photoshop---make unfilled boxes), and then white out the corners.

It was simple, and it took me five minutes to do in Paint. Why can't I do this is the most powerful image editing program available?

In the end, I added 5k to the image size, because the Paint compression utility isn't as good.
