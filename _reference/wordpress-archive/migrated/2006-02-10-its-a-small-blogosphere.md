Today I talked briefly to Darren Herman, who is VP of Business Development at IGA Worldwide, the company that [just raised $12 million](http://www.paidcontent.org/pc/arch/2006_02_06.shtml#053502) in Venture funding for brokering in-game advertisements in upcoming video games.

It's a very cool business with a lot of potential, and I really like the way they seem to be handling it. Their main site is somewhat corporate, but lo and behold: a simple google search gave me [Darren's Blog](http://darrenherman.typepad.com/darren_herman/).

Cool.
