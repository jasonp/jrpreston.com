I mentioned a day ago that I've got some seriously weird issues with image borders with this theme, and I still do. I just spent the entire length of Die Hard 2 trying to fix it using CSS, but I can't for the life of me figure out how to do it.

So I'm giving up and just marking all my image links with the border=0 html property. It's clunky and I don't like doing it, but I give up on figuring out the real method. If anyone knows the secret for this particular theme, feel free to share.
