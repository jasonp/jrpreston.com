It still bugs me that there's no good way to figure out which blogs are at the center of their own niche community.

When I started Flicker two years ago, I knew that there were people out there who'd want to read it. I knew there was a market.

But I had a damned hard time finding out what was at the center of that community. It took about a year of blogging and hopping around people's blogrolls and links to find [AFK Gamer](http://afkgamer.com/), [Destructoid](http://destructoid.com/), [Buttonmashing](http://www.buttonmashing.com), [The Escapist](http://www.escapistmagazine.com), not to mention [Joystiq](http://www.joystiq.com) and [Kotaku](http://kotaku.com).

Now I'm working on another project in an entirely separate niche of the blogosphere, and I'm having exactly the same problem. I know these bloggers and readers exist somewhere. I know that there are probably four or five blogs that are really the epicenter of thought in the subject. But I'll be damned if I know how to find them.

Yes, I've tried Technorati. And Technorati does some things really well, but in my opinion, this is not one of them.
