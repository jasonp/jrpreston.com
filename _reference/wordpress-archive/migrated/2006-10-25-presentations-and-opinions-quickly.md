I've wanted to attend one of these conferences ever since I started working on them almost two years ago. For some reason, circumstances have made it impossible for me to attend until now, and I'm glad I'm here.

It's really great to hang out with a bunch of people who know what they're talking about and have their own opinions about blogs and new technologies.

It's also really cool to see the people I work with up on stage presenting - I'm going to try to get myself up on stage sometime, because I love presenting my outlandish opinions to a roomfull of strangers.
