[[Image: BloggerParty]](http://cesbloggers.com/sponsor-our-2008-blogger-party-at-ces/)Work on a Sunday? Sometimes it happens.

I've just finished going through the outstanding invite requests in my inbox for our [2008 "It Won't Stay in Vegas" CES Blogger Party](http://parnassusgroup.com/2008/01/the-2008-ces-it-wont-stay-in-vegas-blogger-party/), which is going to be *insanely* cool.

The shindig is going down next Tuesday the 8th at the [Atomic Testing Museum](http://www.atomictestingmuseum.org/) just a block off the strip. Getting to the party won't be an issue though, since we've got the Intel Blogger Van shuttling bloggers to and from the Bellagio (South Tour Lobby Entrance) starting at 5:15p.

You can see a partial guest list for the event [here](http://cesbloggers.com/partial-2008-guest-list/): we've got RSVPs from [Scoble](http://www.scobleizer.com/), [Canter](http://marc.blogs.it/), [Gizmodo](http://www.gizmodo.com/), [Major Nelson](http://majornelson.com/)...you get the idea.

We're going to have some awesome music, food and, of course, an open bar. Beyond that, we've got thousands of dollars in schwag and prizes from our sponsors: [Intel](http://www.intel.com) (platinum sponsors), [SimplayHD](http://www.simplayhd.com) (gold), [HP](http://www.hp.com) (gold), [Parnassus Group](http://www.parnassusgroup.com) (gold), and [Broadclip](http://www.broadclip.com) (silver).

If you're looking to get an invite to the gig, we're hosting it for active tech bloggers who are going to be at CES covering the gear. Shoot me an e-mail (jason AT parnassusgroup DOT com) requesting an invite and include your blog URL.

See you in Vegas!
