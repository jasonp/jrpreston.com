I write better posts when I use TextEdit, or Notepad.

Not Word. Word has too many buttons. I'm constantly worried that I might accidentally click something that lowers a disco ball from my ceiling and forces me into a musical number from *Saturday Night Fever*.

There's something about the big empty white box that makes me actually pay attention to what I'm writing. I still haven't decided if that's a good thing or not.

Regardless, my friend Ethan has come up with an excellent post of his own today: [Rules for seeing TRANSFORMERS](http://rabidspacedog.com/2007/07/02/transformers-rules-for-seeing-what-could-be-the-greatest-movie-for-our-generation/). Bearing in mind that Ebert almost gave this movie four stars, you might want to prepare yourself before you get into the theater.
