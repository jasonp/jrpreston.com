After updating the software on my iPod (yet again) in order to get it to talk to the new version of iTunes (which crashes more than past versions, doesn't it?), I am finally trying to sync my iPod and library with all the of things that have happened to it in Europe.

The most frustrating part is this. I have a playlist on my iPod that I made in Europe. Its made up entirely of songs that I have on my computer already, but I can't even *export a list of those songs* to re-construct the list on my computer. Nope. I have to manually go through each of those songs and insert them into a newly created playlist if I want it on my computer.

Stupid fucking iTunes. That's just idiocy.
