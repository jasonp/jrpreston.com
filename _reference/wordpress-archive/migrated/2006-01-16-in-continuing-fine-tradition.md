I've now started the second of two guilds I've ever had World of Warcraft characters in, which makes me either cool or hopelessly afraid of commitment (i.e. unable to actually join someone else's guild).

The first guild was named "Channel 5 News Team", which is pretty cool.

This one is called "Low Class Guild," and it features such excellent ranks as "degenerate," "hooligan," and "habitual drunk." Everyone should aspire to be a habitual drunk.

I've been playing on an entirely new server with entirely new characters (of an entirely different faction) and it's been immesurably fun. I basically started out playing with four or five of my good friends all in the same place at the same level, and really that's what makes WoW so much fun.

I also suffer from an intense desire to play EVE Online, even though I know I don't have time for other things to suck away my life.
