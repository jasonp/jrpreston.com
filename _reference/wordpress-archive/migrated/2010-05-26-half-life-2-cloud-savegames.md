[Half-Life 2 Cloud Savegames](http://store.steampowered.com/news/3872/)

Valve just announced that the Half-Life 2 series is available today on the Mac. Even better, they've enabled "cloud" savegames, where you can pick up, on another computer, exactly where you left off - even if you've swapped from a Mac to a PC, or vice-versa.

From their Press Release:

> The Half-Life 2 titles available now feature cross-platform support for Steam Cloud, allowing players to save their game progress on one computer, then seamlessly resume from that save point on another computer (Mac or PC).
