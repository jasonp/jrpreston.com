![](http://images10.newegg.com/NeweggImage/ProductImageCompressAll125/24-014-108-06.jpg)It occurred to me that one way to circumvent the problem of wanting to have a ridiculously huge TV and a lifestyle that, for the moment, has me driving up and down the west coast all the time is...(drumroll) buying a projector.

I really didn't know that HD projectors could be had for under $1,000 dollars - but apparently it's possible.

After a little bit of research, and spending a long time looking for reviews that don't seem to exist, I think I've mostly convinced myself to grab the [BenQ PE7800 DLP](http://www.newegg.com/Product/Product.asp?Item=N82E16824014108) projector.

If I bought it now, I realized I can use my windowshade as a projector screen until I get back down to Los Angeles...at which point I'd have to drop another couple hundred on something like [this screen](http://www.costco.com/Browse/Product.aspx?Prodid=11071504&whse=BC&topnav=&browse=&s=1). Unless I can use a wall.

Anyone have experience with HD projectors, this one in particular, or advice about it in general?
