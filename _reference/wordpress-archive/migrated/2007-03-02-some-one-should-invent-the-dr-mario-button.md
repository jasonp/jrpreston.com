[Image: dr mario button]Sometimes I don't really understand the way my mind works, but it amuses me, so I keep it around.

I was working on my screenplay when I noticed that [Celtx](http://www.celtx.com/download.html), the awesome freeware program I use to write, has a little button in the toolbar that looks like a Dr. Mario capsule.

Wouldn't it be great if it was a Dr. Mario button, and when you clicked on it it launched a game of Dr. Mario? Talk about a great way to deal with writer's block!

In fact, I can't really think of a program that *wouldn't* benefit from having a Dr. Mario button. Some one go do it!
