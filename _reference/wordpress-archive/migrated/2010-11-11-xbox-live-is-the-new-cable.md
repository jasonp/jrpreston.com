[Xbox Live is the new cable](http://sxsw.com/node/5864)

This from the announcement about Felicia Day keynoting at SXSW 2011:

> In a ground-breaking deal, "The Guild" partnered with Xbox to produce and release seasons 2, 3 and 4 on Xbox Live, while allowing Day to retain the intellectual property rights to her show.

I finally realized: Xbox Live is the future of cable. It's a closed-platform (i.e. easy to develop for), IP-based, interactive link and distribution system directly to millions of people's television sets; it allows for two-way communication, and people already have their credit cards tied to these accounts.

Content is automatically time-shiftable, selectable, chat-around-able...this is brilliant. With Kinect in the picture (heh), you can now offer *video call in to shows* from people's own homes.

Oh. Em. Gee.

Who wants to start the first TV channel on Xbox Live?
