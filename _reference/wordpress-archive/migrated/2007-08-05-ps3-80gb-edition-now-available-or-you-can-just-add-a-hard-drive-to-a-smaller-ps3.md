This is maybe the first Playstation-related release that it is possible to miss. If you live in the US you can now run over to Gamestop and [buy an 80GB version](http://www.gamestop.com/product.asp?cookie%5Ftest=1&product%5Fid=020279) for the original, way-too-much price.

Or, as I just found out, it's entirely possible to just buy a hard drive from newegg and [attach it to any version of the PS3](http://www.gamespot.com/features/6176090/index.html?tag=topslot;title;1). Save yourself some cash and buy a 20GB version from ebay.

Frankly, I got a PS3 months ago and it's basically served its time since I finished *Resistance* as a Blu-Ray player only.
