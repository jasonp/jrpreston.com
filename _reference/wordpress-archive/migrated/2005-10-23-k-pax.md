I just picked up [K-Pax](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/redirect?link_code=ur2&camp=1789&tag=flickergaming-20&creative=9325&path=tg/detail/-/0312977026/qid=1130089240/sr=8-3/ref=pd_bbs_3?v=glance%26s=books%26n=507846) yesterday, and I'm already halfway through it. It's a relatively short book, and so far it seems to be in the same vein as [Ishmael](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/redirect?link_code=ur2&camp=1789&tag=flickergaming-20&creative=9325&path=tg/detail/-/0613080939/qid=1130089368/sr=2-3/ref=pd_bbs_b_2_3?v=glance%26s=books), in that it's a bit of a reflection on the nature of human society. It's less direct, of course.

So far I'm enjoying it immensely, and I'd recommend it. I'd like to see the movie, as well.

\[ Update: I finished the book, and it's definitely a good read. Not something you *need* to read, per se, but definitely worth picking up. \]
