I watched the new episode of Family Guy on TV last night. It's probably the first time I've seen the show on TV in ages. As I've [said before](http://www.jason-preston.com/?p=285), TV scheduling and my lifestyle just don't really match.

In any case it was hilarious. They made a reference to one of the best movies of all time: [Better Off Dead](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0088794/), and it was wonderful. But I watched it with two of my friends who haven't seen that movie.

I would like to put the clip up on my blog to share it with you, but I can't. What I should be able to do is go to Fox online, scroll through the episode I want, and pick at 12-20 second clip to post to my blog.

When you can do that, and it's *easy* to do that, is when legally sharing videos will really take off.
