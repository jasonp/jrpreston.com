I've always wanted to write a blog post on a subway. I don't know why, maybe because it's one of those things that you just can't do in Seattle.

It also seems like the subway is good thinking space.

I'm in New York City this week attending Web 2.0 Expo. A lot of people are arriving today, and I'm looking forward to tonight's ignite talks.

Web 2.0 looks like it's going to be a good event, which is always inspiring to me, and leaves me with plenty of new ideas for our own events and parties, plus I get to see friends that I really only see at these events.

That's it for my subway blogging this trip, I'm at my destination. What a good way to spend the time.
