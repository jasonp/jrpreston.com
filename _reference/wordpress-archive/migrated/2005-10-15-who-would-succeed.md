There are two things that, if they arrived in England, would instantly become so popular that I doubt they could build stores fast enough.

First, Cinnabon. I haven't had a creamy, delicious roll of doughy cinnamon and icing since I left America, and good lord I miss it.

Second, Pumpkin pie. Nobody here has heard of it, and I'm getting increasinly worried that this November will be one horrible pumpkin-pie-less month.
