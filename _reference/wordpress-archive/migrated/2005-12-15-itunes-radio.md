I have only just discovered the fact that iTunes carries radio streams over the internet. I'm sure that this little feature has been part of iTunes for some time, and that my internet connection has been fast enough to carry it, but I've never bothered to actually look through them.

Despite the fact that I have a fair amount of music, I tend to like to listen to music I didn't necessarily select myself. It's always fun to hear something unexpected and cool (which is why random play orders are so cool). But what gets me most is that...these are so much cooler than podcasts. Why? Because they have MUSIC.

Meh.

What we really need are those WiFi city networks, so everything can be replaced with digitalness. Internet radio in my car, please.
