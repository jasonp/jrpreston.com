While I was in the shower this morning I noticed how my teeth feel like they're moving around a bit - anyone who has ever had braces or headgear or whatnot knows that kind of vague pressure that means something is rearranging itself. So I made the mistake of examining my teeth in the mirror a little bit later.

Although nothing, as far as I can tell, has *moved* significantly, the teeth that I'm going to tenatively call K9's (because they look like fangs or something) are, in my mouth, not pointy. Yep, the tips that I thought they had, sicking down from either side of my mouth...not there. It looks like something chopped off the bottom bits.

I have no idea how long they've been like that. I don't really *look* at my teeth that often. I should definitely get a dentist appointment when I go home. Damn this British food.
