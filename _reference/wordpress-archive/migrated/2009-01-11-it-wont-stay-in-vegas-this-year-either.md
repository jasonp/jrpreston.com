Every year my company, the Parnassus Group, puts on the [It Won't Stay in Vegas](http://itwontstayinvegas.com) blogger party at CES, and every year it gets a little more cool.

This year we had over 500 people RSVP (you can see the star-studded list over at the site), and I think at least half of them showed up.

I think everyone enjoyed the Sony gaming lounge--how could you not--and the Lovesac furniture surrounding the consoles provided some really serious lounging cred to the whole outfit.

I know AMD had no shortage of people interested in their new tech, and Sonos provided a really awesome home entertainment system to some lucky bugger.

But I think I might be the one who came away with the real win: Levar Burton signed my laptop.

Yes, that Levar Burton. Yes, my laptop. The incriminating evidence is at the bottom of this post.

[Wil Wheaton](http://wilwheaton.typepad.com), I'm coming for you next! Even if I have to do it at PAX. I know, that's cheating, but STILL.
