Fred Wilson [wrote a post](http://avc.blogs.com/a_vc/2006/04/technical_innov.html) yesterday about Innovation as a driving force in new businesses.  
  
I think he's largely right - many of the most successful business stories are based around re-definig a business model more than a technology. I think of these ideas most often when I think of what makes a "really good business idea."  
  
I also think that the [Bag, Borrow and Steal](http://www.bagborroworsteal.com/) idea Fred mentions is one of those ideas. I think that's brilliant.
