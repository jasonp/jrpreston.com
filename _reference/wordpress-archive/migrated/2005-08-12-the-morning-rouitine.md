As far as newsreading routines go, I'm sure my new habits are a little bit strange.

Mostly, it's a product of Firefox and the fact that I'm in love with tabbed browsing. When I open up my [start page](http://www.start.com/3/), I'll take a few moments to glance around the various rss widgets it sports, and I'll ctrl+click anything that looks interesting (which opens the item in a new tab, behind those previously open). Basically, this let's me "flag" interesting items to read as soon as I'm done doing whatever it is I wanted to do when I opened my browser.
