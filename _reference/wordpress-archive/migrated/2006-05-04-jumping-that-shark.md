According to the Harvard Crimson (Well, I can't link inside facebook!) Facebook has [opened up to business e-mails](http://www.thecrimson.com/article.aspx?ref=513312).

In other words, Facebook is no longer limited to just College and High school students with .edu e-mail addresses, which was one of the things that made Facebook cool in the first place. Let's be honest, it's cool to be part of a club. That's why the Gmail beta worked so well; you had to be *invited*.

But oh well. I think Facebook may have [Jumped the Shark](http://www.jumptheshark.com/). I've said it before, but I'm going to go on record with this: Facebook should have sold last month.
