Interestingly enough, google is blog-searching [through blogger](http://www.search.blogger.com/), not Google itself.

While they lose the Google name-recognition itself, it does fit well into the Blogger service umbrella--I'm not sure I like it though. I was looking forward to having an "rss" tab on the google homepage, not having to go to a separate site.

If Yahoo! ads an RSS tab to their search box, I'll be sorely tempted to switch back to their search.

Start.com needs to get their rss search running, too (although the feeds feature is pretty neat).
