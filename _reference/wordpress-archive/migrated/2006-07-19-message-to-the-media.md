When you lock your archives behind sixteen different walls then all the work you put into them (that is, the sum total of journalism for everything but the past two weeks) is completely unsearchable, unusable, and therefore completely worthless.

You *have to keep the content out there* or it loses any value it once had. Get that through your skulls.
