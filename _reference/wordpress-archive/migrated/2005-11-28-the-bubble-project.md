This is a really cool thing that I spotted in some sort of newspaper a while back. I forget exactly when, but I've been trying to remind myself about it for ages, and finally I'm online when I remember it. It's called [The Bubble Project](http://www.thebubbleproject.com).

How it works is this guy, Ji Lee, goes around posting black speech bubbles over all sorts of advertisements, and then waits for random people to fill in the space. Sort of directed graffiti and social commentary, I guess. But there are some pretty funny and interesting resuts. I've posted a vew below, or you can [browse the whole gallery](http://www.thebubbleproject.com/01.Bubbles/BubblesFrameset.htm) yourself.
