Against all odds, I managed to trick the wireless network here into letting me download with BitComet, because whoever coded that thing also integrated a bunch of IE functions into it so you could browse the web. For some reason, if I do so and download from within BitComet, the 'net here seems to have pre-approved it to transfer files.

The downside is that in order to get all 3.5 gigs of Firefly, I need to sit in the computer lab for about 30 hours for the download to complete. It's a bit depressing to think that by the time I watch the whole season, I'll have spent over a day's worth of time in the lab.

But the first episode -- Serenity -- has just now finished, and I'm about to watch it.
