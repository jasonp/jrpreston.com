I've had a pretty good weekend in Sunnyvale and San Jose (and, briefly, [Half Moon Bay](http://twitter.com/jasonp107/statuses/319085232)), CA. On Friday Steve, Teresa and I flew down to attend Community Next (which was awesome) and Graphing Social (currently going on).

I've spent some time meeting a whole bunch of cool people doing cool things in Facebook and blogging. Hung our for a while with [Nick O'Neill](http://www.allfacebook.com/), our partner in putting together the Web Community Forum, and did some interviews with people like [Craig Ulliott](http://webcommunityforum.com/2007/10/craig-ulliott-on-using-facebook-platform-as-a-launching-pad/) (who is from Brighton, where I spent a semester abroad).

The weather has been nice and the company has been good. I'm headed home tonight. Can't complain.
