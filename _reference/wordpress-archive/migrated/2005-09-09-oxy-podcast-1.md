Despite the fact that the 'cast has been ready for nearly a month, sitting on my hard disk under the "oxy podcast" folder I created specifically for it, I am only just now releasing [the first oxy podcast](/media/opc1-8.2.2005.mp3).

It's got some good stuff in it, so check it out if you've got the time. After being out here for a week or so, I've realized that coming up with a reliably regular show is going to be a bit of a task. I'll do my best to keep them coming regularly, but the ones from out here will probably be a little bit scattered.

If you'd like to simply pop the feed into iTunes and then let technology do the rest of the work, simply pull down the Advanced menu, click "subscribe to podcast..." and enter:

http://feeds.feedburner.com/Delicious/tag/oxypodcast

iTunes will automatically download new podcasts when I release them.

Enjoy, and feel free to send any feedback my way: jason \[at\] flickergaming.net
