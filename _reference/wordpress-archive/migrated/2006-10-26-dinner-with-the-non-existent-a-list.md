Last night I was lucky enough to enjoy a wine and gin tasting dinner at Maggianos with a number of the attendees of the Blog Business Summit.

It was one of the most fun evenings I've had in a long time. We got to taste three different types of (nicer) Gin, and were treated a few stories about the origins of the drink. Apparently, Gin was developed originally as a medicine?

The dinner itself was excellent, but I was most excited to get to hang out with people like [Matt](http://photomatt.net/), [Scoble](http://scobleizer.wordpress.com/), and [Pirillo](http://chris.pirillo.com/). That's not an oppotunity I usually get, and these are some cool people - geeks at heart, like me, I guess.

Matt, in particular, is an extremely cool kid. Everyone should use Wordpress.
