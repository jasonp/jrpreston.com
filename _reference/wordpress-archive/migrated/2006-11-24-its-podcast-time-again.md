[Image: podcast]I managed to finagle myself a spot in [this Thanksgiving's GamerAndy Live podcast](http://www.gamerandy.com/archives/2006/11/gamerandy_live_40.shtml), and it was a boatload and a half of fun.

The team over at GamerAndy are great people, and it was really fun to sit down and chat with them for the duration of the podcast.

If you have an interest in video games and you haven't already subscribed to their podcast, go ahead and put [this address](http://www.gamerandy.com/podcast.xml) into your favorite podcast program (if you're using iTunes, it's Advanced -> Subscribe to Podcast).

And if you're dying for an hour or so of my tinny-little voice and opinionated banter, you can grab [just this episode](http://www.gamerandy.com/podcasts/galiveep065.mp3).
