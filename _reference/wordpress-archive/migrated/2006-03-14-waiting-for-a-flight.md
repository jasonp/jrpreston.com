I'm waiting for my connecting flight here in the Albuquerque airport where my two-hour layover became a lot more fun when I noticed this sign:  
  
"Free Wireless Internet Access Available"  
  
More airports and public buildings should offer free wireless access. In today's world, the internet (and laptops and other Wi-Fi devices) are becoming more and more ubiquitious, and wireless access should become more and more of a publicly available service.  
  
The wireless internet here is provided by the city of Albuquerque. And this is now my favorite airport.
