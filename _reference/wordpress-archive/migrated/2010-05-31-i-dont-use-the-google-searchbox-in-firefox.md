Or any other browser with a built in searchbox. I always type in "google.com" to the address bar and search from Google's own home page.

And the *reason* I go to all this trouble every time I want to search is that it annoys me *to no end* that the last search string is retained in the searchbox after the search is performed.

Why in the world do I want everyone and anyone who looks at my browser or computer screen to see my most recent search string?
