It's the little things that get me at 1:30 in the morning, like not hanging up my clothes.

I don't know why, but that last step of getting pants onto the hangar is the most difficult. I usually get pants off, and folded, but more and more often I'll drop them onto the floor instead of hanging them up.

Normally I wear jeans several times before washing them. You do too. But I bet that the more I leave them folded on the ground instead of hung up in the closet, the less I get to wear them between washes.

Little things. Like flossing. They'll be the death of me.
