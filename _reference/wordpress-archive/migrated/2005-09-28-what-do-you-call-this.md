Jason Calcanis wrote up a little thought list on [where the major internet players are going](http://calacanis.weblogsinc.com/entry/1234000380060529/), and all I can say is "that's damn interesting."

As I worked for the [Blog Business Summit](http://www.blogbusinesssummit.com) over the summer and basically got way too cozy with the blogosphere, I realized how much fun it is to work for a small, innovative business. I really don't know what it is I'd like to do in life, but I do know that it should involve:  

  
-   Writing
  
-   Computers / The Internet
  
-   Inventing

By Inventing, all I really mean is "coming up with new shit, or at least being *with it* when the new shit turns up."

It's funny to me (and probably nobody else, but at least I think it's funny instead of sad) that I created [Flicker](http://www.flickergaming.net) without know about any of Weblog's blogs, and in reality the site I vaguely had in mind to make was, more or less to the T: [Joystiq](http://www.joystiq.com).

At least I know my mind is going the right direction.

So whatever all that is---that's what I want to do!
