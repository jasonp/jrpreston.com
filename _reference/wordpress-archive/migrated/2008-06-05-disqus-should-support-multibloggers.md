I know I am not the only person who blogs in multiple places. Fred Wilson blogs at [A VC](http://avc.blogs.com), [Union Square Ventures](http://unionsquareventures.com), and on a [tumblog](http://fredwilson.vc). I am sure that there are times when he would rather attach his name to one of those places rather than another.

When I comment on blogs about business and facebook, I like to leave the url of the community/facebook blog [Web Community Forum](http://webcommunityforum.com), so that people click back to something relevant.

when I comment on blogs about publishing, i like to leave a link back to [eat sleep publish](http://eatsleeppublish.com), so that people will hopefully find a relevant site they're interested in

when I comment on blogs about videogames, I like to leave a link back to my personal blog, since that's where I pontificate about gaming...

you get the idea.

It would be cool if [Disqus](http://disqus.com) let me select from one profile or another (or simply show all blogs in the drop-down) when leaving a comment on another site.
