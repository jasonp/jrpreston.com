It's not really that rare to have a dream that puts some random sequence of seemingly unrelated experiences together.

But last night was the first time I can remember my dream making a mistake, and then trying to correct it. It's actually kind of funny.

Come August I'm going to be living off-campus for my Senior year of College, and it's the first time I've actually gotten an Apartment and, well, lived off campus.

In my dream last night, I retured to school and was going through the usual process of mundane things in interesting ways that only dreams can concieve of, when, about a day into the experience, I realized that I *wasn't* supposed to be living on campus anymore.

So I spent the rest of my dream trying to contact my new roommate and get keys to the apartment.

??
