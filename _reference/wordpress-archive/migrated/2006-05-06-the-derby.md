![](http://espn-att.starwave.com/media/horse/LawyerRon_060415_i.jpg)Today is [Kentucky Derby](http://www.kentuckyderby.com/2006/derby_coverage/derby_entrants/brother_derek/) day, the first of the triple crown races, and probably the most famous. I always try to watch the triple crown, although I haven't really followed horse racing this year very much. Just glancing over the entrants and statistics it looks like [Brother Derek](http://www.kentuckyderby.com/2006/derby_coverage/derby_entrants/brother_derek/) or [Lawyer Ron](http://www.kentuckyderby.com/2006/derby_coverage/derby_entrants/lawyer_ron/) are the current favorites, despite their bad post position.

I also don't remember past Derby's having 20 entrants...I seem to remember it staying at least under 16 most years. This is a *big* race, and the bigger the race, the more random the whole thing gets.

A lot of people say it will never happen again (and for some good reasons), but I would like to see another triple crown horse show up.
