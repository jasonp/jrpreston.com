I asked yesterday what sites would be replacing tv-links now that nobody can watch House if they happen to miss an episode on Tuesday night. Turns out someone already compiled a list.

[Here's the list.](http://tvteddy.blogspot.com/2007/10/tv-links-replacements.html)

\[ **Update, since this post seems to be wildly popular, and hits pretty high in Google, I thought I'd put a few of the better sites here, so people don't have to click through all the time.** \]

[SurfTheChannel](http://www.surfthechannel.com/) - A pretty legit TV-Links clone. If you knew your way around the old site, this will look familiar to you.

[Alluc](http://www11.alluc.org/alluc/) - Different look and feel, frankly I don't like it as much. But you know, two links is better than one.

[VideoLemon](http://www.videolemon.com/) - pretty simple page, one-column, just go nuts style thing. At first glance, not sure their selection is that huge.

[FindTVLinks](http://www.findtvlinks.com/) - Looks exactly the same as videolemon. In fact, maybe someone just cloned the site at a different address. \*shrug\*

If anyone knows of something completely awesome that isn't on this list, just drop it in the comments.
