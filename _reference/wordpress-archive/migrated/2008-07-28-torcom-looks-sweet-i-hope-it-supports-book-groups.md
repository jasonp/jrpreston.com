I remember signing up for a once-a-week free e-book to promote the launch of [Tor.com](http://tor.com/) a while back because I wanted to snag the sweet stories they were sending out.

And then I forgot about it completely.

But I belatedly noted my good friend [Ethan's post about Tor.com](http://rabidspacedog.com/?p=428) being up and immediately went to check it out. It looks cool, I'm signing up.

I don't know what's in store, but this strikes me as the ideal place to host some online book clubs. I hope their community supports that (and if it doesn't, it should!).
