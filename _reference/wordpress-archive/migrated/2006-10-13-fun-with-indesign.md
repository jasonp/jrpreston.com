My friend [Ben](http://www.adlin.com) has a wondeful knack for doing things that are visually appealing, and for the past year or thereabouts I've been watching over his shoulder as he plugged away at things in Illustrator and InDesign, maddeningly jealous.

Because I like to tinker. And it's damn near impossible to sit around and tinker with InDesign on your friend's laptop, especially when said friend likes to keep his settings intact.

So it was with great joy that I finally got myself a copy of Photoshop CS2 last week, which includes both Illustrator and InDesign, and I tinkered to my merry heart's content putting together a logo and flyer for my newest little project (which, incidentally, I will probably tell you about next week). I'll even let you download the flyer.
