There seems to be [a lot of pressure](http://news.yahoo.com/s/ap/20051201/ap_on_go_pr_wh/us_iraq) on Bush to pull out of Iraq.

Frankly, of everything Bush has done (and is doing) in office, one of the better things is that he's commited to staying in Iraq until it's necessary to leave.

Yes, I know people who are there now. Yes, I know its dangerous. Yes, it's sad to think of my friends or my friends siblings or my fellow countrymen dying in a country halfway across the world in a war they may not have cared about.

But, callous as it sounds, they need to be there. I would rather that they be there. It's not some glory-days-of-war type thinking that leads me there, and certainly there are [experts](http://www.livejournal.com/users/jacobrb02/220107.html?#cutid1) who [agree](http://www.pupress.princeton.edu/chapters/i7833.html) with me.

The basic point is that we really are commited to building a sustainable replacement for the previous sustainable (if horrible) system we knocked over. If we leave too soon because of silly political pressure, BAD THINGS will most likely happen, and who knows, we could end up shooting ourselves in the foot and sending our armies back overseas in less than a year's time.

I think there's a surprising amount of good that comes from US troops and US organizations being there. Like any such undertaking, Iraq has its share of unethical beneficiaries and unnecessary casualties, but overall I think there honestly *is* progress. I really hope we're smart enough to stay until the Iraq we essentially created can hold itslef up on its own.
