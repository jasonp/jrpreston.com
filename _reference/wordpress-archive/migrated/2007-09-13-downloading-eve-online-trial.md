[Image: eve]

Am I making a huge mistake? I've heard from many people that EVE is one of the more life-consuming MMORPGs, and, after all, it was only a year ago that I finally gave up on World of Warcraft.

I should probably just reinstall Freelancer.

But I have a huge, and I mean HUGE soft spot for games that revolve around spaceships. They just look *so cool*. I remember logging hours and hours of *[Escape Velocity](http://www.ambrosiasw.com/games/ev/)*, then [*Privateer*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wing_Commander:_Privateer), then *Privateer 2*, and then *[Freelancer](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freelancer_%28computer_game%29)*.

This is probably a mistake. But it looks so cool!
