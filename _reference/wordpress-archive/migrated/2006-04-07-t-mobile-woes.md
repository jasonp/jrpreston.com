For some reason, T-mobile offers virtually no service where I spend 90% of my time. It's frustrating because the service is cheap and excellent everywhere else - I only need to go about a mile in any direction to get almost perfect service.

So I've kept with it, especially knowing that I'll be living in an entirely different area starting in the next year or so.

But this past week has been horrible - my phone has dropped almost every call it's supposed to have recieved, it refuses to send text messages for days at a time, and won't tell me when I have new voicemails.

This is especially frustrating here where it is literally my only phone - I missed out on a chance to visit a friend since I they couldn't get hold of me.

I'm hoping this is simply the phone being broken, but if it turns out that the T-mobile service has just started to really not work, I might have to switch.
