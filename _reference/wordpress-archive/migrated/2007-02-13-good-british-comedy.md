I have no idea how old this thing is, but it reminds me a lot of the Flying Circus, which is never a bad thing. I particularly love the Office parody stuff with Jesus - pictured above.

I'd embed the video, but sadly it doesn't have that option for some reason. The link is [HERE](http://video.google.ca/videoplay?docid=5252160678533282004).

Go check it out, it's well worth your while.
