The other Jason (OK, so technically *I'm* probably the "other jason") [saw Blade Runner: The Final Cut](http://www.calacanis.com/2007/10/06/blade-runner-night-out/) in Los Angeles the other night, and apparently loved it:

>   
> Blade Runner: The Final Cut was STUNNING last night. I've seen Blade Runner dozens of times on DVD/VHS, but nothing prepared me for the greatness that was The Final Cut. The film looks like it was made in 2007. The images were so stunning that it would seriously win an Oscar for best effects in 2008. The detail level of the images in the background was so great that I found myself drifting off of the characters and into the the details of the buildings, cars, and alleyways.

Blade Runner has always been one of my favorite movies of all time. I wrote several papers on it in college and I never get tired of watching it. I will be very upset if I don't get to see the Final Cut in theaters. (Next time I'm in LA is in November).

Bring this film to Seattle!
