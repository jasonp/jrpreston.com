Last night I was thinking about how the blogosphere is really made up of a bunch of little worlds that people have, and how people don't often jump between those little worlds, finding entirely new spheres of blogdom. So I thought I'd do something to help people break those little spheres, and I think it'll make a pretty decent meme if you'd like to jump on board.

How does it work? Simple: pick a blog you like that's probably outside the radar of most of the people who read your journal, and give it a little love. Ready? Go!

**[Man Bytes Blog](http://blog.pjsattic.com/corvus/)**  
It's odd to pimp a blog that has more readers than I do, but this is probably well out of the normal sphere for those people reading me. Corvus tends to write about ideas like storytelling in games, myth, or game design, but generally in a more in-depth or philosophical way than you'd expect. His posts are well thought out and interesting, and he writes extremely well. For a chance to expand your blogging horizon, you should go [look at his blog](http://blog.pjsattic.com/corvus/).
