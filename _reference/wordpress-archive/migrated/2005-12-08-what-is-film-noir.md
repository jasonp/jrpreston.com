Today I'm turning in the 3,000 word paper I wrote for my *Film Noir* class. It was one of those papers where the prompt was: "think of your own prompt."

So the question I tried to answer was:

*To what extent can the films* Minority Report, Dark City, *and* Blade Runner *be considered part of the film noir genre?*

Overall, I'm pretty happy with the way the paper turned out (unlike the paper for my IR class, which deserves a D at best), so I'm going to post a .pdf copy of the *film noir* paper online, in the exact form it will be when I turn it in, and see how long it takes my professor to accuse me of plaigarism ;)

Download it [here](http://www.jason-preston.com/writing/noir.pdf).
