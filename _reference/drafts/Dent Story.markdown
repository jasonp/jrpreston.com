# The world needs more than ideas

April, 2017

1. People whose areas of expertise are different
2. Shared experience with mild adversity
3. Forced convection
4. Intellectual expectations
5. Open and vulnerable
6. Non-competitive peer group

The central drivers of most serious progress have been diverse, non-competitive communities. French Salons. 

Mega-connection tends to homogeonize. 1950s TV. SXSW. Mega Churches. 

Distributed micro-connection breeds new ideas and deep relationships. Dent. 

The world needs more than ideas. We need relationships and the magic of personal connection; ideas together. 

Obviously Dent serves many of these needs for many people. But you can foster this in your own org: how???
